# Description

![[Pasted image 20260827111421.png|411]]

Elias Blackwood is a lean, wiry man in his early thirties, standing at around 5'11". His dark, tousled hair falls a few inches above his shoulders, often kept tucked behind his ears. His piercing green eyes, sharp and observant, seem to miss nothing, constantly darting about to take in his surroundings. Elias's face is marked by a few faint scars, souvenirs of past encounters and close calls, adding a rugged edge to his otherwise handsome features. 

Elias is rarely seen without his worn, dark brown Stetson-style hat, which casts a shadow over his eyes. His attire is practical and unassuming, favouring dark, muted colours that help him blend into the shadows. A well-worn, hooded cloak often shrouds his form, concealing an array of hidden pockets and compartments filled with the tools of his trade. 

Elias often smokes a pipe, the smoke curling around him as he contemplates his next move. Despite his stealthy appearance, there is a quiet intensity in his gaze.

# Journey So Far

Since joining the expedition aboard the [[The Flor De Mayo]], Elias Blackwood has established himself as the party's investigator, scout and infiltrator. Quiet and observant by nature, Eli frequently finds himself drawn toward anything suspicious, often investigating alone before the rest of the party is even aware that something is wrong. His instincts have repeatedly proven valuable, from following a mysterious figure into [[Lenity Woods]] in the early days of the expedition, to infiltrating the [[The Barnett-Blake Ironworks|Barnett-Blake Ironworks]] and later investigating the increasingly bizarre mysteries surrounding [[Fortuna Outpost]] and [[Underton]].

Eli's skills have developed into one of the party's assets. He is exceptionally capable of moving unseen, frequently making use of [[Sel]]'s invisibility magic to scout dangerous locations or carry out delicate operations. He has demonstrated an impressive ability to read people as well as physical evidence, often noticing subtle changes in body language or behaviour that others overlook. At the same time, Eli tends to distinguish between suspicion and proof, preferring to investigate his instincts rather than immediately accepting them as fact.

His skill with a crossbow has also become a defining feature of his fighting style. Eli has repeatedly demonstrated accuracy, including killing a [[Mutant Lake-Lizards]] with a shot through its mouth and finding creative ways to exploit his surroundings against otherwise formidable enemies. Despite his preference for stealth and distance, he has repeatedly placed himself in danger when the situation requires it, including being knocked unconscious during the battle with the [[The Furnace Spider]].

As the journey progressed, Eli became increasingly involved in the events surrounding [[Fortuna Outpost]]. The strange occurrences there forced him to confront threats far removed from the criminal investigations of his former life. He witnessed supernatural phenomena, the appearance of ghosts and manifestations from the past, the strange temporal disturbances affecting Fortuna, and the machinations of [[Maeve]]. He became heavily involved in investigating the deaths of [[Ares de Vries|Ares]] and [[Braskin Dropstone|Braskin]] and eventually helped uncover the truth surrounding [[Silas Seer]].

His relationship with [[Silas Seer|Silas]] became particularly significant. Although Eli initially participated in attempts to determine [[Silas Seer|Silas]]' involvement in the events at [[Fortuna Outpost]], he ultimately became one of those most determined to prevent [[Silas Seer|Silas]] from being needlessly killed. He helped devise and execute the plan to fake [[Silas Seer|Silas]]' hanging and later argued that he deserved to be treated with dignity rather than being cast aside. His actions suggest that, beneath his pragmatic and suspicious exterior, Eli has developed a strong sense of loyalty towards those he considers companions.

The journey beyond [[Fortuna Outpost]] has only brought stranger mysteries. Eli has repeatedly taken the lead when exploring abandoned or dangerous locations, including the gatehouse and the ruins of [[Underton]]. He has investigated strange creatures, discovered hidden passages, recovered the Sun Key and other artefacts, and searched flooded chambers despite the obvious danger. In [[Underton]] he has become increasingly involved in uncovering the mystery surrounding the town, its inhabitants and the strange forces that appear to have overtaken it.

Eli's journey has also revealed something about the man beneath the investigator. He is pragmatic, secretive and willing to deceive when necessary, but his actions increasingly show a deepening loyalty towards the people around him. His response to [[Wild Bill]]'s transformation in [[Underton]] is perhaps the clearest example of this. After [[Amotti]]'s near-fatal encounter with Bill, the truth of Bill's nature is finally revealed: he is **William Von Zarovich**, descended from parents who were creatures belonging to a cursed cult. Bill admits that he had discovered the truth about his parents and fled from them, only to find himself now beginning to undergo the same transformation.

Terrified by what he has done and seeing the fear on the faces of his companions, Bill attempts to distance himself from the group, telling Eli, _"It's not safe for me to stay here, Eli. You are not safe."_ Eli refuses to accept this. Rather than responding with fear or treating Bill as a monster, he places a hand on Bill's shoulder and tells him:

> _"We all have demons inside us, Bill, some of them are just more visible than others. I know you are a good man."_

The two embrace, and Bill then reveals that, before he fled, **his parents did something to him**. Eli subsequently brings Bill back to the Town Hall and argues that the group should learn more about his condition before deciding what to do with him. When [[Amotti]] protests, Eli compares Bill's condition to the infection they had all suffered at Fortuna, reminding the group that they had faced that together and telling them, in effect, **that they would overcome this together too**. Even when faced with evidence that Bill may be something dangerous and fundamentally different from the man they thought they knew, Eli chooses to see the person beneath the condition.

The moment provides perhaps the clearest indication yet that Eli's loyalty is not merely practical. He is willing to put himself at risk for someone he considers a friend, even when that person has just demonstrated the capacity to seriously hurt another member of the group. It also reveals something about Eli's own worldview: his statement that _"we all have demons inside us"_ feels particularly poignant for a man who has spent the entire journey keeping his own past largely hidden. Whether this is simply compassion for Bill or an indication that Eli understands something about carrying one's own demons that he has never shared with the others remains unknown.

Despite everything that has been revealed about the world around him, remarkably little has been revealed about Eli himself. He has allowed the party to learn fragments of his history, but his life before the expedition remains largely hidden. The more time he spends investigating the mysteries of others, the more conspicuous the mystery surrounding Elias Blackwood himself becomes.

# Backstory

Very little is known about Eli's life before the expedition, although several fragments have surfaced throughout the journey.

The clearest information came when the party discussed their origins before leaving [[Clement's Hollow]]. Eli revealed that he came from **[[Waterdeep]]**, describing his former life as coming from a "different sort of underworld" — one concerned with **investigating and rooting out corruption and criminality within the city**. Exactly who Eli worked for, what organisation he was associated with, and what cases he investigated remain unknown.

His abilities strongly support this account of his past. Long before revealing his history in [[Waterdeep]], Eli repeatedly demonstrated the instincts of an experienced investigator: following suspicious individuals, conducting surveillance, searching locations for evidence, reading body language, noticing inconsistencies and approaching dangerous situations with the expectation that there is something hidden waiting to be uncovered.

There are also several much stranger glimpses of Eli's past.

During a restless sleep at the [[Clayborn Inn]], Eli experienced an unusually vivid dream in which he found himself sitting around a **familiar table in a familiar home**, surrounded by what appeared to be his family.

A tall, lean man with **black hair and bright green eyes** stood by the hearth. His appearance is strikingly similar to Eli's own, particularly his distinctive green eyes. Beside Eli sat a kind-faced blonde woman, showing a young blonde girl the intricacies of jewellery making.

The girl was named **Lyra**.

Lyra had little interest in jewellery and instead dreamed of a life of adventure. Most significantly, she declared:

_"I'm going to stalk the streets like Elias, rooting out injustice wherever it lurks!"_

This suggests that whatever Eli's role in [[Waterdeep]] was, **Lyra knew of it and looked up to him**, aspiring to follow the same path.

Another young woman, **Amara**, was also present. She had raven-black hair and striking green eyes and appeared considerably more stern than Lyra. She reprimanded Lyra for her naivety and scolded her father for indulging her ambitions. This establishes at least some familial connection between Amara, Lyra and the father figure in the house, although the precise relationship between these figures and Eli has never been explicitly explained.

The scene was interrupted by an increasingly ominous **knocking at the door**. As the knocking became more disturbing, the room and the figures began to swirl and distort until Eli awoke suddenly.

Whether this was a genuine memory, a dream, a supernatural vision, or a distorted combination of the three remains unknown.

The experience is particularly significant because it is the only extended glimpse so far of a possible family life belonging to Eli. The people in the vision have never been properly identified, and neither Lyra nor Amara has appeared by name elsewhere in the story.

Eli has also experienced several other unexplained encounters that may or may not be connected to these memories. While travelling through the canyons, he briefly perceived a humanoid figure with large, round brown eyes watching him from the canyon wall, only for it to disappear when he looked again. Later, at [[Fortuna Outpost]], he briefly saw a **young blonde girl** running past the well before she disappeared. Whether this girl was connected to Lyra is unknown, but the similarity has not gone unnoticed.

These incidents occurred during a period in which the party was increasingly exposed to supernatural manifestations and visions connected to people's histories, making it unclear whether Eli was experiencing memories from his own past or becoming caught up in a wider supernatural phenomenon.

The scars on Eli's face provide another small clue. They are described as **"souvenirs of past encounters and close calls"**, suggesting that his life before the expedition was dangerous, although no specific incident has yet been associated with them.

For now, the known pieces of Eli's past amount to a handful of fragments: **a mysterious family, a familiar home, a young girl named Lyra who admired him, an older woman named Amara, a man who may be his father, a life spent fighting corruption in [[Waterdeep]], and an ominous event behind a door that Eli has never explained.**

The circumstances surrounding these people, what happened to them, and how they relate to the Elias Blackwood who eventually boarded the _[[The Flor De Mayo]]_ remain among the greatest unanswered questions surrounding him.
