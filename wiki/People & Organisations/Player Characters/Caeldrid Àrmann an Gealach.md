# Journey So Far

Caeldrid arrived in [[Clement's Hollow]] as a quiet human Eldritch Knight — a farmer's son from a struggling village, drawn to adventuring less by ambition than by need: his people rely on the resources and coin he sends home. His name carries the weight of an older, moon-touched tradition, and he speaks often of the "laws of hospitality" as sacred, rebuking [[Gambit]] and [[Amotti]] when they steal from Issy. From the first carriage ride into town, he took the mission seriously while the others gawked at their strange new home. His bond with his owl familiar Gwynevere — who simply appeared to him one day, unexplained — hints at something mystical stirring under his practical, warrior's exterior.

Across Chapter 1 in Clement's Hollow, Caeldrid grew into the party's steady hand. He shore off a three-headed lake-lizard's tail with a green-flame blade at the Western Gate ([[Session 4]], dove into[[Lake Lenity]] to drag a drowning [[Sel]] from the Lord of the Lake's corpse and cut the black Fragment from him under moonlight (Session 10), and after [[The Furnace Spider]] fell he took custody of the icy-blue Hollowsteel Blade that has been his signature ever since. At the Checkmate Door he and [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] traded blows with [[Ignatius 'Nate' Barnett|Ignatius]] and the [[Fire-opal Golems]] to buy the others time to flee. In [[Session 24]] he made the campaign's hardest call — over [[Amotti]]'s protests, he worked [[Magnolia 'Old Mags' Aurix|Old Mags]]'s keys through the lock and freed Ignatius, because the situation demanded pragmatism over punishment. That decisiveness in tactics contrasts sharply with his hesitation in the heart: he nearly slipped out of town without saying goodbye to [[Hattie Clayborn]], and only her catching him at the inn door produced the kiss that closed out Act One.

Chapter 2 tested him on the road to [[Fortuna Outpost]]. In the dust-dweller caves he faced the corrupted shaman [[Brez-ak]] in single combat, took a staff-blow that shattered his jaw and sent his teeth flying, and — after Sel reversed time and returned him whole — feinted with his gauntlet, drove his sword up through the shaman's ribs, and twisted (Session 28). It is perhaps the defining moment of his martial career. He wrestled a Chronovore off Sel in the prairie (Session 29), and on arrival at Fortuna Outpost was the one who noticed the garrison was drastically under-manned — the tactical instinct that opened the door on the conspiracy there. The chapter's cruelest beat came in the vision at Session 40, when a corrupted Caeldrid ran Wild Bill through with his own blade; even as an illusion, it left a mark on both men.

In Chapter 3 he has been a pillar rather than a spotlight — slotting hands onto statues in the Ceremony Room of the Archive, nearly falling to a Reanimated Protectorate before Gambit's revolver saved him (Session 48), and finally, in the Session 53 finale beneath Underton, taking the possessed *Curse of the Vampyr* from the air when Lythara's strike failed to end it. He carried it into the tunnel, dropped it on the earth, lit his sword in blinding white flame, and pinned the book to the ground through the jewel on its cover until it burst into smouldering ash — freeing Gambit and destroying an ancient evil in one stroke.

As a character he is the party's moral centre: loyal, deliberate, romantic without being soft, and reliably the first to step forward when someone needs shielding. His strengths are mechanical (a magical blade that bites supernatural things, flame-wreathed strikes, Gwynevere's scouting) and personal (courage, hospitality, an unglamorous sense of duty). His weaknesses are the mirror of those virtues — emotional hesitation in the face of anything he can't solve with a sword, a preoccupation with home that keeps part of him elsewhere, and a susceptibility to the same corruption that has claimed better men. At the end of Session 53 he stands victorious but unresolved: far from his village, still working to keep it fed, the question of Hattie still open, the mystery of his owl still unanswered, and the Hollowsteel Blade in his hand now proven, blood and ash and all, against the very worst the world can throw at him.
