# Overview

Name recalled by [[Sel]] during a strange dream while asleep in the [[The Barnett-Blake Ironworks|Barnett-Blake Ironworks]] lounge in [[Session 18]].

The whereabouts and identity of this figure are as yet unknown.

In [[Session 54]], Sel experiences a vision of his past in which he relives a memory of Zelraun:

> Beneath his feet, Sel sees fallen leaves. Some the colour of amber. Some the colour of yellow fire. Some the colour of burnt cinder toffee. He looks to his sides and above and sees trees start to appear, colossal Yuirwoods with trunks as wide as towers. Their canopies ascend beyond vision into a dewy fog. Sel follows the path before him. Eventually, he sees a faint plume of chimney smoke rising from up ahead. It is coming from a simple wooden cottage. He approaches, and sees a man standing on its porch, nonchalantly leaning over the balcony and smoking a long, thin wooden pipe.
> 
> He is slim and lithe, with wavy brown hair that frames his angular face. It looks somewhat greasy and unwashed. He also has a thin moustache and goatee. He wears a dirtied white shirt open at the collar, and a pair of rough cloth trousers held up by braces. Around his neck, a pair of brass, telescopic goggles  hangs. He puts them to his eyes to better see Sel as he approaches. When he moves, he does so slowly. Deliberately. Sel recognises this man. Zelraun.
> 
> As Sel draws nearer, he looks down at his hands. They are soft-skinned and pallid lilac-grey in colour. He looks up, and Zelraun removes his goggles. He smiles, and licks his thumb before using it to put out his pipe and wiping it on his trousers. He looks at Sel with two glinting eyes of granite-grey and a wry smile underneath.
> 
> *"And where have you been hiding? Your notes are up to date, I take it? We're finished now for today in any case. Don't bother preparing supper for me tonight, but I will need you to pack my things. I'll be away for a few weeks."*
> 
> Sel moves past him and through the door to the cottage. The man follows and continues to speak, distractedly gathering some things scattered around the room.
> 
> *"I'm off to Candlekeep, they've finally granted my access request. There are answers in that library, somewhere, I'm sure of it. While I'm gone, don't do anything I wouldn't do. And certainly don't do anything that I would."*
> 
> Sel nods, and makes his way to a set of stairs he knows from memory are at the back of the room. As he begins to descend, his vision fades and he hears a last word from Zelraun behind him.
> 
> *"Don't worry.. even if someone were to find this place, I've made the incantations and the shield guardian is in place. Besides, I've seen you training, you can handle yourself."*
