# Roles & Relationships

Former owner and barkeep of the [[The Canary's Rest Saloon]].

# Description

He is an older, smooth-talking human with slicked-back grey hair and a walrus moustache. His arms and chest are lined with tattoos representing Sirens of ancient myth.

# Overview

Mentioned in the journal of [[Mayor Jacques Colliere]] found in [[Session 49]] as being in possession of one of six [[Underton Town Hall|town hall]] vault seals. Although not a Council Member in his own right, Dion received the seal of Chief Engineer [[Tenby]] from Colliere, as Colliere had suspicions that Tenby was on to the illicit spore trafficking happening in [[Underton]].

The Players meet Dion under strange circumstances during their mysterious experience in the Canary’s Rest Saloon in [[Session 55]]. 

> *“I’m a barkeep, friend, forgetting is what I’m selling.”*
