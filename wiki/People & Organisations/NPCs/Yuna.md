# Description

Yuna is a wizened gnome of wispy grey hair and pointed chin. He wears rings on his fingers that are actually the repurposed nuts and bolts of pieces of machinery.

# Overview

Former associate of [[Gambit]] and [[Edward]] mentioned by Edward during Gambit's dream in [[Session 18]].

In [[Session 55]], Gambit has a vision of a critical moment from his past involving Yuna.

Yuna nurses Gambit back to health after the incident with the [[The Dodecahedron|Soulstone]] at [[Greed]]’s mansion. He struggles to fight tears while trying to explain to Gambit, whom he knows as Tidus, that he must now leave Faerun and go on the run. Yuna gives Gambit his mechanical arm, a final parting gift. He then stays behind to await and stall the parties that will be hunting Gambit - [[The Crimson Syndicate]] and Greed himself.

> *"Here is what you must do, my dear boy. Forget the past. Forget me. Forget Edward. Run. Run as fast and as far as you can. They will come here first. It’s too late for me, now. But I can do something. I can help you. (He pauses to hold back tears) Raising you and your brother has been the privilege of this old gnome's life. Now… over there, look at the board. Those are all the contracts we have. Any one of them will grant you passage out of here. Listen to me carefully. I must have plausible deniability when they get here. Do you understand? Go, now. Take a contract at random, make sure I can't see which one. Go where it says and get far away, as quickly as you can.”*
