# Overview

A gold elf from Faerun, and the most famous acolyte of [[Strahd von Zarovich]]. He spent a century living with Strahd, and is said to have fed on Strahd's blood and welcomed the curse of the vampire to his own flesh willingly. He brought the curse of the vampire to Faerun, and it was from him that it spread throughout the land. At some point in forgotten history, he turned against his former master and vanished from Faerun - never to be seen again.
