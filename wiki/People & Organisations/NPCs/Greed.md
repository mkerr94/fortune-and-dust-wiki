# Overview

Mysterious figure mentioned by [[Yuna]] during [[Gambit]]’s vision of his own past in [[Session 55]].

A person of considerable power. Gambit and his brother Edward stole an artefact known as [[The Dodecahedron|the Soulstone]] from his mansion prior to the beginning of the story. Edward was killed in the process and Gambit tried to use the Stone to revive him.

Yuna warns Gambit that Greed will hunt him relentlessly in pursuit of recovering the Stone.
