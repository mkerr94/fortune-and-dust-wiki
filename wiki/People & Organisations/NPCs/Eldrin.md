# Overview

Brutal former commander of [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] and Chief Psionic Biologist of [[The Kaliesh’erai]], first seen during a vision of the past experienced by Lythara during [[Session 55]].

Eldrin was in pursuit of power over life and evolution using the psionic power of his order. He unsuccessfully tried to recruit Lythara as an enforcer for the organisation, one who would wield the power and unleash it on the world.

Lythara refused and fled the stronghold of the Kaliesh’erai, with Eldrin vowing to pursue her.

> *“This will be the last mistake you ever make, Lythara.”*
