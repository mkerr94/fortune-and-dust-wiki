# Roles and Relationships

Sister of [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] and [[Lyra]].

# Description

A serious and pragmatic young woman of startling, verdant green eyes and raven-black hair. 

# Overview

Sister of Eli, first seen during a nightmare experienced by [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] in [[Session 7]] and then mentioned again during a vision of the past in [[Session 55]].

Prior to the beginning of the story, Eli suspected that Amara had been kidnapped by [[The Crimson Syndicate]] and embarked on a campaign of revenge against them in [[Waterdeep]].
