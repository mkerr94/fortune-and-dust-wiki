# Overview

The Players learn from Agatha in [[Session 44]] that Herny has been taken by what Agatha calls [[The Nightcrawlers]]. She intends to rescue him before escaping Underton.

In [[Session 49]], the Players learn from the journal of [[Mayor Jacques Colliere]] that Henry was missing for a time before things went bad in Underton - possibly through the fault of the Mayor himself. He returned claiming to have found a new seam for the mine, and soon after rose in station as new discoveries were made as a result.

# Description

Henry is described as the same height as Agatha. A stocky man with glasses and a bristly moustache on his upper lip.

# Roles

Former School-master of [[Underton]] and husband to [[Agatha Winslow]].
