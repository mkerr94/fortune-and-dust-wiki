# Overview

Public figure of [[Underton]] referenced in the journal of [[Mayor Jacques Colliere]].

Likely connected to the Underton mine.

In [[Session 52]], the Players discover that Granville Milton is the Underton Mining Co. Overseer, and a member of the town council.

In [[Session 54]], the Players find Milton dead - trapped in a secret tunnel beyond the hidden underground lab in the Mining Co. headquarters. Next to him, they find his Town Hall vault seal. They also recover from his hand a sample of the refined fungal spores. They surmise that, in his bleak final moments, he chose to seek refuge in the comfort of the spores rather than face the terrible fate that had befallen him.

# Description

He is a short, round man who wears a rough-spun suit too short for him at the extremities. He has mole-like pointed features and wears fingerless gloves from which grubby hands poke out.
