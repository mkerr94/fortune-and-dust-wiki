# Roles and Relationships

Younger sister of [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] and [[Amara]].

# Description

An inquisitive, bright and headstrong young girl of tumbling blonde curls and eyes of sea-foam blue.

# Overview

Younger sister of Eli and Amara first seen during a nightmare experienced by Eli during [[Session 7]].

She helps her mother make jewellery in their home in [[Waterdeep]], but longs to fight crime and injustice like her brother.

> *"I'm going to stalk the streets like Elias, rooting out injustice wherever it lurks!"*
