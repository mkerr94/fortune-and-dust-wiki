# Overview

Former Mail-keeper of [[Underton]]. He cared for the [[Chiropterrings]] and oversaw the sending and receiving of correspondence.

The Players open a safe belonging to him in the Underton aviary in [[Session 44]]. Inside, they find a triangular, metallic object that appears to be one part of a greater whole.
