# Overview

The Players find his body in [[Session 49]], halfway through transformation into one of [[The Nightcrawlers]].

The Players find his journal, detailing the events of the days leading up to his death.

16th Eleint - Henry came back. Missing for nearly 24 h and just turns up, at the saloon. You could've scraped Dion's jaw off the floor. Worst part was, there was no anger, no shame. The guy is infuriating. Not only that, but he’s suggesting a direction for a new coal seam. With [[Granville Milton|Milton]] right there! Watching that jumped-up mole getting hot under the collar was amusing at least. If Henry says he’s found something, I say let’s give it a try. Worst case scenario him and Milton end up with egg on their face. Still, I wonder if we perhaps did take it a little too far with him the other night. Boys will be boys I suppose. Anyway, it should stop Agatha coming here every hour to ask me what happened.

18th Eleint - That son of a bitch. He was right. Milton says he made it look like a collapse, guess he didn’t want to be shown up by the fucking schoolteacher in his own backyard. Time will tell, but he says there is something back there, deeper than the other seams. Guess we’ll just have to let Henry into the club, for now.

20th Eleint – This is going to be delicate. No coal at the new seam, but the fungus down there is growing more wildly than any other bloom we've found. This is going to send spore production through the roof. The Viper will be pleased, but there is the problem of Henry. And the miners. We're going to have to be more careful than ever. Not only that, but Milton says they've found some Quager relics, mage-craft of some sort he thinks. Moron wouldn't know mage-craft if it cooked his breakfast. It's set [[Gabriel]] off though, preaching now about the Founders old connection with the Quagers. That Henry has been given a gift from the Craftsman himself. Servant save me. Still, that stuff could be of value, and could give us an excuse to get to the spores. Whatever they manage to dig up, we'll put in the vault for now. Safest way.

22nd Eleint – Henry is trying to get to me, I'm sure of it. The way he looks at me, he knows what we did that night. Bastard. He's too far in now, he basically ran the fucking meeting yesterday. I should've known he'd get above his station, always been desperate for approval that one, it's pathetic. Will need to appease him for now, he's seen too much. I've seen him and Gideon talking. They're going to try and cut me out, I know it. Maybe I'll raise concerns about them to Samosetia. I wouldn't imagine [[the Emerald Viper]] takes too well to those too ambitious for their own good.

23rd Eleint – I've split the seals to the vault among the Council Members. Well, all but that buffoon [[Tenby]]. He's started asking questions about the weight of the coal exports we're shipping out. Fool should stick to coal goes in, steam comes out if he knows what's good for him. I've given his seal to Dion at the saloon instead, safer that way. Tenby won't be happy though and we need his train. Will need to figure it out. Made a nice ceremony of it, real pious. Six seals, six Founders, it's Gabriel's wet dream. Only one place to put mine anyway. No way [[Violet]] will get her hands on it there, I know she's been drinking on the job.

24th Eleint -  Had dinner with Henry and his wife tonight. Agatha. More resourceful than she looks, that one. I don't trust him as far as I could throw him, but will have to keep them both sweet for now. Maybe if I could get him one on one...

Last entry (undated, scrawled and tearing through the paper with the force of the quill) - [[The Mother]] and her hive will rise from the depths to consume the lands of light.

# Description

Colliere is a plump, balding man in fine clothes. When found, he appears to have committed suicide by revolver.

# Roles

Mayor of [[Underton]] (deceased).
