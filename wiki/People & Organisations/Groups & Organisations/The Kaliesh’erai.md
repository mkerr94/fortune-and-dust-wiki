# Roles and Relationships

Secretive order of high elves researching the use of psionic power to control life and evolution itself.


# Overview

Under the guidance of their Chief Psionic Biologist [[Eldrin]], they conducted monstrous experiments on living subjects in the dungeons of their stronghold.

[[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] fled from this order after refusing to consent to her role in their work.
