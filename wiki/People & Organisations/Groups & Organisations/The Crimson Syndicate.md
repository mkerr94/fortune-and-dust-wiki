# Roles and Relationships

Shadowy criminal organisation somehow linked to the pasts of both [[Gambit]] and [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]].

# Overview

Gambit’s guild was hired by the Crimson Syndicate to steal the [[The Dodecahedron|Soulstone]] from [[Greed]]’s mansion.

Eli crossed paths with the [[Waterdeep]] element of the Crimson Syndicate before coming to [[Alcadeña]]. He suspected them of having kidnapped his sister, [[Amara]].
