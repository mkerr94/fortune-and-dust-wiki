# Overview

One of the group of figures referred to in [[Alcadeña]] as "The Founders."

A statue in the [[Underton Town Hall]] Ceremony Room depicts the Sorcerer as a scruffy halfling female. One of her hands is outstretched, palm facing forward, and covered in arcane symbols.

# Description

The Sorcerer's symbol is the Arcane Hand.
