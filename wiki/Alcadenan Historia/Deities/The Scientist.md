# Overview

One of the group of figures referred to in [[Alcadeña]] as "The Founders."

A statue in the [[Underton Town Hall]] Ceremony Room depicts the Scientist as a wiry gnome male with endlessly searching eyes who hold before him a lantern which houses a small flame.

# Description

The Scientist's symbol is the Captured Flame.
