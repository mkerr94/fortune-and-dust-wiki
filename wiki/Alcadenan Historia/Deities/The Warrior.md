# Overview

Deity-like figure worshipped by [[The Protectorate]].

One of the group of figures referred to in [[Alcadeña]] as "The Founders."

The small shrine in [[Fortuna Outpost]] depicts The Warrior as a steadfast figure in full armour, holding a downward-facing sword with an ever watchful eye at the centre of its hilt.

# Description

The Warrior's symbol is the Watchful Sword.
