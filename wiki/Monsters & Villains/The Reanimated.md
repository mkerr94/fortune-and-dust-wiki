# Overview

Reanimated corpses encountered throughout Chapter 3 by the Players.

They appear to have no instincts of their own, simply mindlessly and relentlessly attacking any who approach.

They seem to be little affected by physical damage, however recoil when exposed to fire or bright light.

[[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] discovers that one of the bodies, that of a young woman, appears to have been forcibly burst open from the inside out of the back.

[[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] notices several small, dark shapes scuttling away from the corpses when defeated.
