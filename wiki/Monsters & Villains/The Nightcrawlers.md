# Overview

Mysterious creatures that have devastated [[Underton]] and killed or taken its former inhabitants.

They are seen by [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] through the eyes of Gwynevere in [[Session 44]] and are described as at least 10 ft tall, with bird-like legs and movement but insectoid features.

Some facts about them are revealed to the Players by [[Agatha Winslow]] in Session 44.

> *“It don’t matter if you can fight. Them Tecs sent a whole bunch of soldiers here out of Fortuna, and look what happened to them."*

> *“Started with people acting strangely, you know? Like they wasn’t themselves. Strange atmosphere settled in, but none of us saw what was coming next. Folk started disappearing. Vanished, right outta their homes. Screams in the night. That didn’t last long. Soon after that the nightcrawlers came streaming outta the tunnels, ravaged the whole place. It was… a lot of people died. Friends of mine, torn apart. And my Henry snatched up. Been quiet ever since. Save for at night."*

> *"They only move at night. They came out of the mines, far as I can tell. I don’t know for sure but I know that’s where they go back to in the day and that’s where they take folks. They’re using the tunnels, see. It’s the light, the sunlight. They hate it. Soon as sun comes up they scatter like rats in a grain house. Only thing that’s kept me alive, that and the birds.”*

[[Elias Blackwood|Eli]], [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]], [[Wild Bill]] and [[Gambit]] encounter and kill and injured nightcrawler in the Underton station house during [[Session 45]]. They see that the skin is a pale grey, but appears to darken when in shadow. 

# Description

The creature has multi-panelled insectoid eyes and a set of wings on its back. It attacks using its razor-sharp claws and a deadly stinger on the end of a long tail.
