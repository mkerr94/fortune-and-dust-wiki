# Overview

Mysterious creature that stalks the murky waters of the flooded Lower Archive in the [[Underton Town Hall]].

In [[Session 49]], [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] narrowly escapes its jaws while retrieving the sculpture of the Opened Book from the Lower Archive.

# Description

The creature resembles a colossal, bottle-green eel with opaque white eyes and rows of disjointed, needle-like teeth.
