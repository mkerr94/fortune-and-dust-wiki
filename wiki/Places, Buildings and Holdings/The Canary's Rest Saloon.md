# Overview

[[Underton]]'s saloon, located on the third level of the town near the Mining Co. Headquarters.

Ramshackle in its construction, it appears to occupy two floors. A short set of steps lead up to a porch with a wooden balustrade. Above the door, creaking in the stale air, a sign reads: *"The Canary's Rest"*.

In [[Session 54]], the Players enter the Canary's Rest to find a sight they did not expect:

> The saloon doors swing closed behind you. Somewhere in the room beyond, a woman plays piano and softly sings to the crowd. This and the warm, yellow glow of gas lamps welcomes you to the Canary’s Rest. Looking into the room before you, you see circular tables and stools dotted like islands in a sea of lacquered oak. On them, distracted patrons chatter like birds in a cage, dissecting the day’s labours over a game of dice or a shared bottle of whiskey. Doors at the back of the room swing open and closed to the rhythm of the music, a serving man ferrying plates of simple fare out to the clientele. Following the sound in the air, you see a raised platform to your right where sits a glistening black piano. On a stool in front of it, a woman with hair as black as the polished surface of her instrument plays for the gathered drinkers.
> 
> On your left, a smooth wooden bar stretches out in a gentle curve. Empty glasses glint like stars upon its dark sky, catching the light of the lamps. Next to it, a staircase slopes upward to a balcony. A beautiful woman of tumbling red hair and cushioned bust rests a hand on the banister, looking out with a smile to the room below.  Behind the bar, a barman idly polishes a whisky tumbler with a dishcloth and smiles to himself.
