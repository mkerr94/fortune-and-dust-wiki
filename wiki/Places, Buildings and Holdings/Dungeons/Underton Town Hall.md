# Overview

Dungeon explored by the Players in Chapter 3 "They Remember the Dark".

The Underton Town Hall is a large, imposing building of made of stone foundations and repaired in places with modern wood and corrugated iron. In [[Session 45]], [[Agatha Winslow]] remarks that the Town Hall is the oldest building in Underton, and that some in town say it may even have been built by the Founders.

# Layout

## 1st Floor

### Main Foyer 1F

- Cavernous main hall entered by two double doors from the outside. The floor has an engraving of a six-spoked ship's wheel at its centre.

- It is lit by a gas chandelier in the ceiling.

- There are two dead [[The Protectorate|Protectorate]] soldiers sprawled on the floor.

- Doors lead off from this floor into the West and East Wings.

- Twin marble staircases curve upward to a second floor. On the right staircase there is the body of a dead creature.

- There is an ornate, wooden reception desk occupying a semi-circle between the staircases. In it are set three drawers marked in turn with a sun, moon and star motif. They are empty.

- Behind the desk and between the stairs is the entrance to the Town Hall vault, requiring six seals to open.

### West Wing Corridor 1F

- Accessed via a door covered by a destroyed iron shutter from Main Foyer 1F.

- The gas lamps lining the walls seem to have been extinguished here, and water drips from the ceiling and pools on the floor.

- At the far right end of the corridor from the entrance, there is a set of stairs leading to the upper floor.

- The Players find the stairs to the upper floor blocked by a makeshift barricade of barrels and furniture. They fight and defeat [[the reanimated]] corpses of three Protectorate soldiers sprawled on this barricade. They then find that the stairs lead to the West Wing Balcony.

- At the far left end of the corridor from the entrance, there is a door to the Council Chamber 1F.

### Council Chamber 1F

- Accessed via a door from West Wing Corridor 1F

- Door is decorated with Moon motif, requiring Moon Key to open

- Contains ornately decorated fireplace which is warmly lit upon entry. [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] sees that the mantlepiece is adorned with indentations in the shape of a sun, moon and star. She and [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] figure out that the decorative star on the **Star Key** Eli found fits the matching indentation on the fireplace, but requires removal from the key before inserting.

- Contains circular council table with six chairs

- Contains body of young woman, hollowed out from the inside. She wears a pendant chain around her neck. On this chain Eli finds the **Star Key**.

- Contains one of the creatures, which attacks the Players on entry.

- The fireplace reveals a hidden crawlspace leading to [[Mayor Jacques Colliere|Mayor Colliere]]'s Office when the symbols from the three keys are placed correctly

### East Wing Corridor 1F

- Accessed via door covered by intact iron shutter - Players fail to forcibly open and cannot locate mechanism to release shutter.

- Secondary access from stairs from East Wing balcony 2F

- Corridor lit by gas lanterns burning in alcoves, inside one of which Eli finds the **Captured Flame**

- Door to the Ceremony Room 1F - **opened using the Star Key**

- Caved in door to the Assembly Hall 1F

### Assembly Hall  1F

- The lower floor of the assembly hall is furnished by rows of pews facing a raised platform at the far end.

- Beneath the benches, there is a mural depicting a forest, the space between the surface and beneath the soil and a subterranean city populated by catfish.

- Various parts of the mural are illuminated when apertures on the ceiling are opened using the chains on Assembly Hall 2F.

## 2nd Floor

### Main Foyer 2F

- Upper floor with perimeter balcony looking down on Main Foyer 1F.

- There are doors leading into the upper West and East Wings.

- There is a landing at the top of the twin staircase. In the rear wall there is another door, larger than the others and made of metal.

### West Wing Balcony 2F

- Upper floor with perimeter balcony looking down on Main Foyer 1F.

- There are two doors leading to Archive (lower) and Archive (upper) fed by an enclosed metal spiral staircase - both bearing the Sun symbol. Water seeps from beneath the lower door.

- In a display case at the end of the balcony, the Players find t**he Arcane Hand**.

### Central Upper Balcony 2F

- Landing fed by the twin staircases which connects the West Wing Balcony and the East Wing Balcony

- Against the back wall, there is a sealed iron door decorated with the symbols of the Founders. This door leads to Mayor Colliere's office and is locked from the inside.

### East Wing Balcony 2F

- Upper floor with perimeter balcony looking down on Main Foyer 1F.

- There is a door leading to a staircase feeding the East Wing Corridor.

- The Players find a grandfather clock here with a missing weight and the **Sun Key** as its minute hand. After retrieving the weight from the Assembly Hall 2F, the Players place it in the clock and collect the Sun Key as a reward.

### Assembly Hall 2F

- The upper floor of the Assembly Hall has seating with a view of the lower floor platform and a balcony containing a glass display cabinet within which rests the **Clock Weight**. This item is recovered when entering the correct code into the codex below.

- The cabinet is sealed by a dial-based codex, the solution of which is linked to three chains which open circular apertures on the ceiling, illuminating sections of the Assembly Hall 1F mural below

### The Archive (Lower and Upper)

- Two floor library lined with book shelves, with a balconied upper floor and a flooded lower floor.

- Lower door blocked by pile of Protectorate bodies.

- Hole in the ceiling where something has broken through.

- Flooded lower floor has a dead Marismente, and is stalked by [[The Lurker]].

- Eli retrieves the Opened Book from a lectern on the lower floor which he later finds contains the Moon Key.
