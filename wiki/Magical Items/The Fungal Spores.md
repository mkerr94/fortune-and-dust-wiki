# Overview

Refined fungal spores found in a hidden laboratory beneath the [[Underton]] Mining Co. headquarters by [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] in [[Session 53]].

The Players find several clues throughout Underton that these spores were being illicitly harvested from the mine and refined into a powdery substance. In the hidden laboratory, they discover that the refined powder allows one to experience visions of their own past, that of others exposed to the substance and that of the fungus itself. They find a scientific hypothesis that this may be due to the spores allowing brief connection to the collective mind of a single, ancient fungal entity. 

They also discover that repeated exposure to the refined material causes muscle decay and emaciation, but denies the victim death.

In [[Session 54]], Lythara forces [[Amotti]] to try some of the refined spore-dust, intending for him to see the truth of what became of [[Granville Milton|Milton]] in the hidden tunnel below the Mining Co. Headquarters. However, Amotti instead sees a vision of his own past, from when he and [[Maeve]] were just children. The group then wonder if the effect of the spores changes from use-to-use, and how reliable that makes them as a tool knowing the risks of prolonged use.

For those facing a grim future, these visions of the past must seem a warm comfort indeed.
