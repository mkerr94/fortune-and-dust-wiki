# Overview

Book found by [[Gambit]] in [[Session 51]] in the Upper Archive of the [[Underton Town Hall]].

It is bound in black leather, with pages of yellowed parchment. On its cover, there is a small jewel of deep crimson. It sits above an etching depicting a bat with wings outspread.

In [[Session 53]] the Players discover to their horror that, ever since Gambit found the book and had it drink of his own blood, it has been draining his blood and life-force. This causes the pages to become engorged and swollen with Gambit's blood, and for him to develop a trance-like fascination with the book. When the Players try to destroy it, it responds violently and causes Gambit and [[Wild Bill]] to leap to its defence under the influence of a dark power. Eventually, [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] and [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] are able to destroy the book with magical fire. roy it for good using fire.

# Contents

Its pages initially blank, Gambit is compelled to dab the jewel with his own blood, which reveals its magically hidden contents.

He finds the following information within:

**Vampiric Traits**

- Undead Nature - they do not need air, sleep or food, save for the blood of others

- Shape Changing - they can transform into bats or clouds of black mist

- Animal Familiars - they have command over animals of the night such as wolves, rats and bats

- Charm - they can bend the minds of others to their will

**Vampiric Vulnerabilities**

- Wooden stakes - if pierced through the heart

- Sunlight - burning to the skin if direct

- Running water - they cannot pass this and are harmed by its touch

- Invitation - they cannot enter the homes of others without being welcomed in

**Strahd von Zarovich**

- A vampire lord from the mists of time, whose stronghold was in the castle of Ravenloft in Barovia

- His was the original Curse of the Vampire, and he gathered many followers to his cult

**[[Jander Sunstar]]**

- A gold elf from Faerun, and Strahd's most famous acolyte

- He spent a century living with Strahd, and is said to have fed on Strahd's blood and welcomed the curse of the vampire to his own flesh willingly

- He brought the curse of the vampire to Faerun, and it was from him that it spread throughout the land

- At some point in forgotten history, he turned against his former master and vanished from Faerun - never to be seen again
