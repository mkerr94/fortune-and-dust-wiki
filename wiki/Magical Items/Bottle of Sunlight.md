# Overview

Magical liquid encased in thick, brown bottles by the Players in [[The Abandoned Gatehouse]] in [[Session 41]] and [[Session 42]]. Unknown use and power.

Through using it on a creature in [[Session 46]], [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] discovers that, when smashed, the bottle emits a burst of blinding sunlight which appears to significantly weaken the creatures plaguing [[Underton]].
