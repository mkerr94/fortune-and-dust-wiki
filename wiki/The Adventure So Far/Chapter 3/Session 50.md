# The Events of Session 50


*Previously, in the Underton Aviary...*

[[Wild Bill]]is changed. Standing to his feet and turning slowly, he leaps toward [[Amotti]] with fangs drawn, overcome by a sudden insatiable hunger. Recoiling and confused, Amotti pushes himself against the back wall of the chiropterring cage and tries to talk Bill back to his senses. As Bill approaches, the scene becomes a twisted echo of their confrontation in [[Fortuna Outpost]], when Amotti was under the influence of [[Maeve]]'s infection. Bill grabs him and sinks his teeth into Amotti's neck. Amotti feels the lifeblood drain from him, seeing Bill's veins swell and his skin darken with sanguine power.

In a panic, Amotti casts the room into magical darkness and pushes Bill away with a telekinetic shove. He tries to fly away, but is caught by a slash from Wild Bill's claws as he tries to escape and he clatters into the caged partition, unable to see in his own darkness. He scrabbles for the door to the cage as he hears Wild Bill sniffing the air, hunting for his scent and heartbeat. As Wild Bill draws near enough to reach out and grab Amotti, Amotti's hands stumble backward and he finds the cage door. He moves through as fast as he can just as one of Bill's claws slams into the floor where he was a moment ago.

Amotti draws his rapier and rattles it along the cage bars, sending the distracted [[Chiropterrings]] into a swirling panic. Bill's senses are muddled by the new smells and sounds around him, but he grabs a chiropterring from the air and the creature's shrieks are silenced by a sickening crunch as Bill closes his jaws around it. Seizing the opportunity, Amotti runs for the trapdoor and scrabbles with the mechanism. Unable to open it without being able to see, he drops the magical darkness. He turns to see Bill's now jet-black eyes meet his. As Bill's footsteps approach from behind him, Amotti fumbles with the trapdoor entrance and is finally able to swing it open. The burst of sunlight from outside causes him to wince. Behind him, Bill's eyes are struck by the light and they roll over in his head. He falls to the floor, unconscious.

---

*In the present moment...*

Wild Bill begins to come to, wincing as his eyes adjust to the light. He props himself up and looks ahead to Amotti, who is kneeling by the open trapdoor and looking at Bill with exhausted terror. Before either can act, the chiropterrings swarm out of the open cage and begin to attack Amotti. He struggles to fend them off, and Bill tries to help him by distracting them with some worms from the feed jar. Before Bill can reach him, however, Amotti jumps through the open trapdoor and slams it behind him. He casts magical darkness on the room above, plunging Bill into impenetrable black. Bill scrapes for the door, but is unable to find it. The chiropterrings now turn their attention to him and begin to surround him. Eventually, his hands find the feed jar, and he scatters worms about the entire aviary. With the creatures distracted, he finally escapes through the trapdoor and pursues Amotti.

Amotti runs for the [[Underton Town Hall]], limping and injured. Behind him, he hears Wild Bill calling his name. He reaches the building, climbs the steps, and pushes open the doors. Meanwhile, Wild Bill picks up a trail of Amotti's dripping blood, and pursues him to the Town Hall.

In [[Mayor Jacques Colliere|Mayor Colliere]]'s office, Eli pushes open the entrance door to find an expectant [[Sel]] and Ralf waiting on the other side. Acknowledging that they have yet to find the remaining statue pieces, they search the room more thoroughly. On Colliere's desk, beside a pool of spilled red wine, they find the white marble statuette of the Offered Chalice. Only the Watchful Sword remains.

The Players discuss at length the areas of the Town Hall that they might have overlooked, clues to the last statuette that they may have missed. [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]], recalls the way in which the [[The Protectorate|Protectorate]] corpses who sat in the Assembly Hall were positioned. On the rows of pews, almost in reverence to the raised platform at the far end of the room. The group return there, and indeed find the Watchful Sword placed ceremoniously on the altar there. With all of the pieces in hand, the Players return to the Ceremony Room and place the final two. As they do, the chest at the end of the room clicks open, and inside they find Mayor Colliere's vault seal.

The group return to the Main Hall, only to find an exhausted and wounded Amotti standing at the threshold waiting on them. He staggers toward Gambit and drops into his arms, losing consciousness.

> *"Don't…. trust…. Bill….."*

[[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] goes outside to check if Amotti was followed, and sees a similarly exhausted but much less wounded Wild Bill standing in the dusty street outside the Town Hall. He gestures to [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] and the two ask Bill about what has happened between him and Amotti. Wild Bill explains that, from his perspective, he woke up in the aviary to see Amotti being attacked by the chiropterrings. He then followed him to the Town Hall but is confused as to what is going on. Eli perceives him to be telling the truth. Subtly, Caeldrid releases his grip on the hilt of his sword.

Meanwhile, Amotti shows the others the two puncture wounds on his neck, saying that Bill attacked and nearly killed him. The sight of this wound triggers Sel's memory. He remembers a simple bet made with Bill in the [[Clayborn Inn]] back in [[Clement's Hollow]] and the prize of learning his true name. Wild Bill enters with Caeldrid and Eli, and Sel demands that he identifies himself. Bill is taken aback and speechless, but Sel persists and names him.

> *"William Von Zarovich… Vampire…"*

Bill does not know the meaning of the term. Sel explains that he is one cursed by blood-hunger, an undead monstrosity that feeds from the vitality of others. Bill protests, and comes clean with some details from his past. He explains that his mother and father were creatures. They were part of a cursed cult. When he found out, he left and never returned. This cannot be happening to him. Memories come flooding back in fragmented flashes to Bill, of his attack on Amotti and his transformation. Seeing the fear on his companion's faces, Bill leaves the Town Hall. Eli catches up to him and puts a hand on his shoulder.

> *"It's not safe for me to stay here, Eli. You are not safe."*

> *"We all have demons inside us, Bill, some of them are just more visible than others. I know you are a good man."*

He and Bill embrace, and Bill makes one more revelation.

> *"Before I left. My parents. They did something to me."*

Eli takes him back to the Town Hall, and argues the case to learn more about Bill's condition before casting him out. Amotti protests, but Eli likens it to the infection of Maeve's making that they all experienced in Fortuna. They came through that together and they will overcome this together. Sel relents, and points out that Wild Bill seems to occupy a space somewhere between vampire and human, and seems to be his human self currently. He agrees that more is needed to be known, and remembers a book that may have the answers - Curse of the Vampyr.

Wondering if it may be in the Town Hall Archive, he sends Caeldrid and Amotti to search for it.
