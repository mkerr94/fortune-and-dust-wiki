# The Events of Session 52

[[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] surveys what he can of the lower levels of the town through Gwynevere's eyes. Among the smaller shacks, toolsheds and outhouses of the level below the [[Underton Town Hall]], she spots two buildings that stand above the rest. Both are identified by signs above their entrances, one reading *"Underton Mining Co. Headquarters"* and the other *"The Canary's Rest Saloon"*. Deciding to head first for the Mining Co. offices, [[Wild Bill]] instead states his desire to check on Rusty Jack. [[Gambit]] offers to accompany him, and the Players part ways to pursue their separate objectives.

[[Elias Blackwood|Eli]], Caeldrid, [[Sel]], [[Amotti]], [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] and [[Silas Seer|Silas]] head for the Mining Co. offices. Descending to the third tier of the town, they get a better sense of its geography. As if carved into the hollow left by a colossal drill bit, the levels of the town spiral down to a conical point occupied by the dark pit of the central mine shaft. As the levels go deeper, so the pale dawn sunlight is obscured by the lip of the upper tiers and the lower town is cast in a sickly half-light. The group reach the Mining Co. Headquarters, and survey the two-storey building before them. It is more modern in its construction than the Town Hall, but still set in stone foundations. The upper portion of the building is constructed of wood reinforced with plated iron sheets in places. Offering to scout ahead, Eli slips through the open main entrance and disappears inside.

Eli finds himself in a small reception foyer. There is a desk ahead of him, behind which a wooden door gently rocks on its hinges. To his left, there is a corridor which turns off and leads to another part of the building. He first explores the room beyond the door behind the reception desk, first gently pushing the door open to peek inside. He sees a records room, lined with filing cabinets and shelves in uniform rows. From behind one shelf, a pair of feet lying with toes pointed up stick out ominously. Eli cautiously approaches, and sees that they belong to a body lying on the floor at the back of the room. Its stiffened arms clutch what looks like a ledger, and Eli grimaces when he sees that the head has forcibly been removed at the neck. Holding his breath, he slips the ledger from the corpse's clutches and takes it with him.

Next, he moves down the corridor to the left of the reception and past the door he finds at the end. He emerges into a cavernous planning office, lined with rows of small square desks at regular intervals. In front of him, at the back of the room, there is a set of wooden stairs that lead to another door at the top. There are also doors to his left and right on either wall of the office. The floor here is littered with scattered papers and upturned furniture, but Eli is relieved to see that it is mostly lit by rows of windows from the open upper floor.

He reaches the door at the top of the rear stairs, and finds it locked. Taking out his thieves tools, he attempts to pick it. Each click of his tools echoes round the planning office with an eerie reverberation. Just as he is about to abandon the attempt, the door clicks open. He sees that the room on the other side is an office of some sorts, plushily furnished, and devoid of occupants. Eli returns to the others and leads them inside.

The group explore the office at the top of the stairs. They again see signs of it having been left in a hurry, scattered papers and an open, empty iron safe on a cabinet against the rear wall. On the floor, Caeldrid finds a small pictogram. It depicts six men standing on the steps of the Town Hall. The first is a plump and smarmy figure that Caeldrid recognises as [[Mayor Jacques Colliere]]. The others, he does not recognise. One is short and round, with dark pointed features almost sticking out of the photograph. He wears a rough-spun suit, too short at the extremities, and his grubby and dirtied fingers poke out of fingerless gloves. The figure next to him is thin and wiry, bespectacled and cold-faced. The next is tall, broad-shouldered and gruff-looking, with an unkempt beard. He folds strong arms across his chest. Next to him, a sheepish-looking man with a balding head and round glasses stands slightly behind the others. Finally, a stick-thin man in shoddy grey robes looks out grimly from behind matted locks of long hair. The Players notice that the balding man has been almost scratched out of the photograph.

Finding nothing else in the office, Eli reads the ledger he found in the records room. He fails to pick out anything unusual about it other than that it lists the monthly staff and equipment costs for the past year, and passes it to Sel for a closer look. Sel notices that, in the past month of Eleint, there was an unusual spike in both overall budget available and expenditure - all attributed simply to a project called *"Geological Survey"*. The group deduce that this is likely linked to the new coal seam and 'spore production' referenced in Colliere's journal, and that the Mining Co. was receiving funds from elsewhere to excavate it. They wonder if this is linked to the figure only referred to as *"[[The Emerald Viper]]",* and trade theories about the possible identity of this person. Eli recalls that [[Eva Vanderbilt]], the girl they are looking for, was described by [[Jebediah ‘Jeb’ Clayborn|Jebediah Clayborn]] and [[Agatha Winslow]] as being of strangely green-coloured skin. Could she herself be the Viper?

Still thinking on this, the Players and Silas explore the rest of the rooms available to them. They begin with the room on their left as they head down the rear stairs. It is the miner's recreation room. It is simply furnished with stools, wooden chairs and tables. There are facilities for pulling drinking water and preparing meals on the benches around the walls. The place has again been upturned, with papers scattered on the floor and chairs lying on their sides. On the central table, the Players see a game of dominos which has been abandoned mid-round. Among the papers on the floor, Amotti finds a half-written letter, which reads:

> *"Dearest Carla, Something is happening to the others. I don't know why, but it's too late for us now. They've locked us in and won't say why.  I'm sorry for everything. If this gets to you, you must send help as soon as you…"*

The rest of the letter has been ripped away. Pushing some of the debris to the side, Caeldrid notices fingernail-deep scrape marks in the floor, as if someone had been dragged out of the room against their will.

Meanwhile, Wild Bill and Gambit reach Rusty Jack, restless but alive and safe in the Coal Track train car. Gambit offers his support to Wild Bill in helping him to figure out the changes he is going through, and Bill thanks him gratefully. Looking at Gambit, Wild Bill notices that he is slightly pale and looks exhausted, with dark shadows beneath his eyes. Asking if he is ok, Gambit admits he does feel tired, but chalks it up to restless dreams caused by the sounds he heard in the Town Hall at night. Wild Bill asks if, later and while in company, he might take a look at the pages of Curse of the Vampyr, and Gambit agrees. Their conversation again turns to Amotti, and Gambit expresses his doubt that Bill will ever win his trust back.

> *"I don't want his trust, Gambit, I just want to make sure he ain't gonna do something he's gonna regret."*

Wild Bill and Gambit take Rusty Jack back to the Town Hall, with the intention of hiding him in Mayor Colliere's office. However, they are alarmed to find the door swinging wide-open, and signs inside that the room has been ransacked. Books have been torn from the shelves, items from the desk have been scattered and tapestries have been pulled from the walls. Someone came here looking for something. Gambit asks if it might have been Agatha, but Wild Bill looks around and finds that the bar used to secure the door is missing. Whoever did so clearly meant to see that the group could not take safe refuge in the Mayor's Office again, and knew they were there. Gambit, reasoning that the only other person they have met in town is Agatha and that they have been gone for just over an hour, comes to a chilling conclusion.

> *"Bill, someone is following us…"*

Forced to take Rusty Jack with them, the duo head off to meet up with the others at the Mining Co. headquarters.

In the recreation room, Caeldrid follows the scratch marks on the floor. He sees that they lead off back toward the main entrance. While following them, something else on the ground catches his eye. Although torn in places and roughed up, he sees that it is a sub-surface survey map of the Underton mine network:

(see: [[Underton Sub-surface Survey Map]])
![[underton_mines_map.png]]

The group study the map, and note that ventilation shafts appear to lead out to some of the buildings associated with the various council members. They also note with a chill, that one area of the mine has been circled in red ink, with no other explanation than a scratched message written on top of the map:

*"THEY REMEMBER THE DARK"*

As the group look at the map, Wild Bill and Gambit arrive in the planning office and reunite with the others. Bill delivers the news that their refuge has been disturbed. Sel thinks on this, and notes that Eli did leave a note advertising their whereabouts in the Aviary. The group discuss where they might now rest when darkness falls. Amotti suggests that they use his Adequate Abode to hide themselves in the centre of town. That way, from a position of relative safety, they could monitor the comings and goings of [[The Nightcrawlers]] and whatever else might be hiding in the shadows. Eli poses an alternative idea. Given that the entrance to the Abode would appear to an onlooker as an opaque barrier, they position it just inside Colliere's office - blocking the main door. This way, if someone were to disturb the door in the night, they would be able to see whoever it was and potentially catch them off guard.

Thinking on this proposal, the group explore the only route they haven't yet investigated in the Mining Co. Headquarters. They go through the door on the other side of the planning office, and find that it leads to a set of stairs descending below ground to a basement floor. At the bottom, they find the entrance to another room. Before them is a solid stone wall, with a metal door set within. The door has only a simple pull-handle, and a plaque on its face reads *"Geological Survey Room".* Eli, anticipating a risk of spore inhalation, covers his mouth and nose with his neckerchief and pulls sharply on the handle. The door doesn't budge.

To either side of the door, the Players find some objects of interest. On one side, there is a clipboard log of entrances and exits of various personnel to the room beyond. Caeldrid notices that, for the majority of the past year, only two people have come and gone from the room on a steady basis. However, in the last month, these names have been replaced by new staff. The last entry, dated 1st Marpenoth, Highharvestide day in Underton, logs these people as having gone in but not come out. One of them, Caeldrid recognises as [[Granville Milton|Milton]], listed in the ledger he found in the Town Hall Archive as the Mine Overseer. They see for the first time that Milton is his surname, his first name being Granville.

To the right of the door, the players see a strange set of buttons arranged in three grids of nine squares each:


![[geological_survey_room_buttons.png]]

Eli tries and fails to press some of the buttons in a way that might open the door. He finds that he can press twelve buttons down and have them stay depressed. However, when he presses a thirteenth, the mechanism resets. The group deduce they will need a code of some sort and try to recall if they might have missed one elsewhere in the building. Sel asks Caeldrid to see the pictogram he found again, and turns it to look at the flip side. On this side, in the top left corner, is a small note reading *"345"*.

Wild Bill proposes that this might mean the solution requires pressing three buttons on the top grid, four in the middle, and five in the bottom - totalling the twelve permitted before the mechanism resets. The group ponder how they might solve this puzzle as the pale morning sun crosses the sky outside and shadows stretch in the dust above them.
