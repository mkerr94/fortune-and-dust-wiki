# The Events of Session 51

[[Gambit]] decides to accompany [[Amotti]] and [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] to the Archive to search for a copy of [[Curse of the Vampyr - Book|Curse of the Vampyr]]. Meanwhile, with the day's sunlight fading, [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] asks [[Wild Bill]] if he believes the Aviary to still be a place of safe refuge for the night. Bill shakes his head, and Eli suggests instead that they hole up in [[Mayor Jacques Colliere|Mayor Colliere]]'s office. Eli wonders, however, if they should go and find [[Agatha Winslow|Agatha]] first for her safety. Bill, still absorbing the revelations about his nature, expresses doubt that the others are safe sharing the same space with him. Eli theorises that his transformation was an extreme emotional response, and that while he is calm it may be safe enough. He then leaves to return to the Aviary and find Agatha.

Gambit, Caeldrid and Amotti reach the Upper Archive. With apprehension, they survey the flooded floor below, cautious of disturbing the Lurker. Unknowingly mirroring the conversations happening in the Main Hall, Gambit asks Amotti whether they can continue to trust Bill.

> *"I don't think I can ever trust him again, he nearly killed me. He's a beast."*

Pondering this response, Gambit retorts that Amotti owes Bill some grace after the incident with [[Maeve]]. The trio split up to search the shelves of the upper floor.

Investigating the shelves, Gambit finds that (while most are filled with fairly modern ledgers and accounts) some of them are marked with the symbols of the Founders seen elsewhere in the Town Hall. He approaches one marked by the Opened Book of the Scholar and begins to browse the volumes kept there. He has Ralf lift him to a shelf containing books whose titles begin 'C', and (surprisingly easily) happens upon one titled Curse of the Vampyr. He sees that the tome is bound in black leather, with pages of yellowed parchment. On its cover, there is a small jewel of deep crimson. It sits above an etching depicting a bat with wings outspread.

Meanwhile, Amotti searches another shelf marked with the Arcane Hand of the Sorcerer. He finds a curious book, simply bound, whose cover is marked with a black scorch mark. Unable to determine its title or decipher the scribblings therein, he pockets it for further investigation. On the other side of the Archive, Gambit studies Curse of the Vampyr. To his confusion, he finds that the pages are completely blank. He thinks on this for a moment, and his mind is drawn back to the red jewel on its cover. Almost without thinking, he draws his dagger and pricks his thumb. Pushing down gently to swell a shiny drop of deep red blood at its tip, he then presses it into the jewel. Its shine fades. The colour darkens as it absorbs Gambit's blood. Suddenly, in a flash of crimson, the jewel shines once more. From its centre, a bright red line moves to the bat symbol below, and outlines its stretched wings in scarlet brilliance. Gambit opens the book once more, and finds its pages now filled with scratchy handwriting.

Meanwhile, Eli reaches the Aviary. He sees that the trapdoor is open. As he moves below the ladder, his foot stumbles on something. On the ground, is a discarded shotgun. Eli recognises it as Agatha Winslow's. He ascends the ladder, and finds the Aviary empty. The chiropterrings are gone. He returns to the ground, and looks for signs of a trail. He sees that the ground is disturbed as if from someone lying on their back. To the side, he sees a trail of footprints in the muddy ground leading east, toward the dark mouths of the eastern mine entrances. With the light fading fast, he decides to return to the Town Hall. Before doing so, however, he retrieves some canvas and paint from the Station House. He writes a note and tucks it inside the Aviary trapdoor before closing it.

> *"At Mayor's Office, cum for safety - Eli"*

In the Mayor's Office, [[Sel]] drags the deformed body of Mayor Colliere out into the Main Hall and props it up against a wall. Searching their new refuge to make sure of its safety, he sees again with curiosity that the space is lit by burning gas lamps. He explores again the Mayor's desk, and finds a strange note. It lists various locations in the building, all with a line through them. At the bottom, it says "Main Desk", with no line crossing through the text.

Gambit, Amotti and Caeldrid return to the Mayor's Office. In front of the group, Gambit relays some of his findings from reading Curse of the Vampyr. Although he admits he doesn't understand all of the information, he explains to Bill some of the characteristic traits of a vampire described in the text. He says that they are undead shape-shifters, known for their charm and command over animals. They are vulnerable to sunlight, wooden stakes, running water and cannot enter a home without first receiving invitation. Bill points out that he meets almost none of these criteria, and feels at his neck for a pulse.

> *"Tell me this, feller, how can I be some undead monstrosity if I got a pulse. I got a heart that beats warm, feel it."*

He reaches out to grab Amotti's hand and Amotti recoils. Bill returns to his corner of the office.

Gambit then recounts some of the vampire-lore described in the book, including the mention of the names [[Strahd von Zarovich]] and [[Jander Sunstar]]. Something stirs in Sel's memory. His mind wanders back to the bookshelves of a man intent on immortality, and hours spent studying there. Sel recalls aloud what he remembers about Strahd von Zarovich and Jander Sunstar.

Strahd was a vampire lord, from the deep mists of time. He resided in the land of Barovia, in the stronghold of Ravenloft, far from Faerun. His story is so old that it has faded into myth, a dark spectre from the past. Jander Sunstar, a gold elf, was the most famous acolyte of Strahd's. He welcomed the curse of the vampire on himself, and brought it back to his homeland of Faerun. From this origin point, the vampiric curse spread throughout the land, hidden in the shadows and whispered terrors. Recorded history tells that a rift developed between Strahd and Sunstar, and that the latter turned against his former master. Afterward, all tell of him vanished from Faerun and what became of him was lost to time.

Hearing this from Sel, Wild Bill thinks back to his past. His memory is blurred and hazy, but he recalls a candlelit space. He sees the obscured figures of his parents standing before him, their arms outstretched in welcome, and a barely visible symbol above them on the wall. He struggles, but he can remember no more detail than this.

Gambit, Bill and Amotti debate the trigger for Bill's transformation. Sel steps in, and he and Amotti argue about the similarities between his and Bill's situation. Both burdened with dark family names they did not choose, both courting the edges of evil within their blood. Amotti moves to protest, but is interrupted by the return of Eli. Eli tells the others about Agatha, and with nothing to be done they decide to rest for the night.

Before they do, Sel shows the others the note he found in Colliere's desk, and they once again look over the Main Hall reception desk. They notice that the Sun, Moon and Star drawers have insets for each corresponding key, and they place the keys there. Upon doing so, a hidden drawer opens. Inside, they find a bottle of whiskey marked with the Crescent Moon and Star symbol of the city of Portumluna. Alongside it, there is another note.

> *"Don't think I don't know this is here, Violet! - Colliere"*

![[crescent_moon_and_star.png]]

*Above: The Crescent Moon and Star symbol of the city of Portumluna. A symbol of ship travel, trade and merchants used throughout Alcadeña.*

Slightly disappointed with the nature of their find, the group decide they can at least take some comfort in the drink and return to their rest, passing swigs of it around. Sel identifies Amotti's new book as the [[Evoker's Field Journal - Book|Evoker's Field Journal]]. It contains arcane instruction for the spells Magic Missile, Scorching Ray and Fireball. Eyes closing, Eli notices Amotti's restlessness.

> *"I think we are safe in here, Amotti."*

> *"You think so, but are we safe from each other?"*

In the night, Gambit thinks he hears scraping and movement from within the Town Hall walls, but cannot confirm if this is only those phantom sounds which perturb one in the night when alone in the dark. Those sounds without a cause, without an explanation, but that are enough to set the mind wandering to unseen evils in the dark corners and empty rooms beyond your door.

Morning eventually comes, and the group decide to search for the remaining vault seals. Eli reads the ledger found by Caeldrid in the Archive and identifies the Underton Council Members and their whereabouts:

Tenby - Chief Engineer - Station House

Gideon - Mail Keeper - Aviary

[[Granville Milton|Milton]] - Mine Overseer - Underton Mining Co. Headquarters

Colliere - Mayor - Town Hall

Winslow - Schoolmaster - Schoolhouse

Gabriel - Priest - Bathhouse Cave Temple

Knowing they have found the seals of Gideon and Colliere, and that Tenby's seal was given to the saloon owner Dion (as detailed in Colliere's journal), Caeldrid sends Gwynevere up to scout for the buildings where the seal-holders resided.

As the last of the shadows flee from the pale dawn light, somewhere a clawed hand retreats into the deep tunnels of the mines. Waiting, once again, for the dark.
