# The Events of Session 44

From the roof, [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] whistles to [[Wild Bill]] to catch his attention. He hears a noise on the roof behind him and turns to catch a glimpse of movement. He decides to investigate and looks out over the town from his high vantage point. Beyond the bodies, he sees a vast chasm cut out of the ground. It spans for hundreds of yards on either side and around in a wide circle. Some way off to his right Eli sees what looks to be a curving step of steps leading downward. They lead to a plateau below on which another large building sits. The chasm continues downwards for at least a mile, beyond Eli's sight.  Different levels have been cut out of the ground and are connected by a network of stairs. Several other buildings occupy the spaces where the ground has been levelled out. On the surrounding walls, Eli notices the dark entrances of multiple tunnels which lead directly into the rock face itself. Directly below him, [[Amotti]] walks among the bodies beneath the welcome gate, and notices that they have been torn as if by animals. Looking at the building behind him, Amotti sees that some wooden steps and a porch lead to a large set of double doors, the handles of which have been bound by an iron chain. The windows on either side of the door have been boarded up with wooden planks from the inside.

Investigating the roof further, Eli finds a loose sheet of corrugated iron that, when lifted, reveals a hidden entrance to the building below with a ladder leading down to its upper floor. He descends but his foot slips, and the noise of his fall echoes around the empty building. Eli hears a noise from behind him, the click of a revolver hammer. A voice, female and wavering speaks out.

> *"D…d…d…don't m…m…move! Stay right where you are!"*

Eli raises his hands in surrender. Before him is a slight woman in her mid-forties. She has a thin face and keen brown eyes, reddened and bagged by lack of sleep. Her mousy brown hair is held in place by a pin. She wears a simple red frock and shin-high boots. She also has a satchel containing tools. She carries a double-barrelled shotgun and a sash of shells around her shoulder, as well as the revolver pointed at Eli's face.

> *“Were you followed? Did anything trail you here?”*

> *"No ma'am, far as I can tell - but my group is outside."*

> *“Where have you come from? This place is a death trap, you shouldn’t have come”*

> *"What happened here, where is everyone?"*

The woman sidles nervously to a small gap in the wall, still holding the revolver in her shaking hand and looks out through the planks to observe the fading light.

> *"[[The Nightcrawlers]] is what happened. We don't have long, you say you got people with you?"*

The woman lowers her gun and introduces herself as [[Agatha Winslow]]. She leads Eli to the bottom floor of the building, where she removes a barricade from one of the windows and Eli ushers the rest of the Players inside. Looking around, they see a floorspace which has been cleared. It is flanked by two sets of stairs leading to a balconied upper floor - one set of which has been destroyed and is unusable. Rows of desks which presumably once occupied the ground floor space have been pushed to the walls, with some having been broken down for wood. There is a mural on a wide, framed canvas on one of the walls. It has a tear through it and large sections are missing, but the Players make out that it once depicted child-like drawings of fields, wheat and crops. In the centre of the floor, they see a strange contraption. Moving closer, they recognise what look like the missing sections of the mural. They have been cut and arranged on wooden frames into a windmill wheel, half-constructed and incomplete. Agatha, distracted and twitchy, addresses the group.

> *“Have you seen my [[Henry Winslow|Henry]]? Same height as me, stocky fella, glasses. Bristly old moustache on his upper lip. They took him from me, the night-crawlers, but I’ll get him back. Then I’m getting out of here.”*

The Players try to press Agatha for more information, but she insists that they get somewhere safe first.

> *“I’m sleeping in the aviary, with the [[Chiropterrings]]. I ain’t sure why, but the nightcrawlers don’t seem to like the noise they make. Keeps ‘em from trying to get in far as I can tell.”*

Wild Bill reassures her that the group can handle themselves, and can protect her from any threat that may be coming, but Agatha scoffs.

> *“It don’t matter if you can fight. Them Tecs sent a whole bunch of soldiers here out of Fortuna, and look what happened to them. After they arrived I saw some of them head toward the [[Underton Town Hall|Town Hall]] and some just tucked up and ran. Ain’t seen a peep of any of them since save for what’s out there in the square. Only thing we can do is hide and survive.”*

Urging the group to follow her, Agatha leads them through a hidden exit at the rear and toward the adjacent aviary. They see that the aviary is a building suspended on four iron limbs and elevated from the ground. An iron ladder leading from the ground to the centre of the building’s floor ends in what looks a trap door sealed by a latch. Agatha leads the Players up the ladder, with Eli going first. She shifts back the latch of the door with a grunt and pushes the trap door upward. She clambers inside and disappears for a second before her face reappears over the lip of the opening.

> *“Well feel free to take your chances out there if you like.”*

Below the aviary, Wild Bill and [[Gambit]] realise that they are going where the horses can't follow. Agatha sombrely explains to them that the animals are unlikely to survive the night but at Bill's protest suggests the best thing they can do is to try to hide them in the Station House, the building they have just come from. He and Gambit place the horses inside, and Gambit sets a trap near the door. They fortify it as best they can in the time they have. Wild Bill approaches Rusty Jack for what might be a final farewell. With Gambit looking on, a strange power emanates from Bill's hand. Before his eyes, Rusty Jack turns invisible. Gambit is stunned.

> *"How did you do that?!"*

> *"I ain't sure feller, it just happened. I can only pray it keeps him safe."*

The rest of the group follow Agatha into the aviary. Climbing the ladder and entering the room above, there is a musky smell of animal. They see that the room is split into two parts. The rear half of the room is blocked off by a cage of iron bars with a locked door at the centre. Beyond it, several small dark shapes shuffle from side to side in the shadows. Every now and then, one yawns and reveals two rows of razor sharp teeth which glint in the light. The Chiropterring.

The half of the room the group occupy has a desk and chair, with shelves above with various jars and containers. There is a smell coming from one in particular which, upon inspection, seems to contain multiple large worms in some sort of preserving liquid. The desk beneath is bare but has two drawers on either side. Against the far wall are a series of square openings arranged from floor to ceiling and labelled A - Z. There seem to be some letters resting in some of the compartments. The opposite wall has a basic bedroll pushed up against it with a small gas lamp resting nearby. Agatha walks over, picks up the lamp and lights it. She rests it on the desk before opening a large sack next to the bed roll. From it she produces some hunks of bread and strips of dried jerky, she offers some to the group.

> *“What I could scrape from the General Store. It’s running out but I’m happy to share it with company. Clean water’s the bigger problem. I can’t hardly make it down to the Bathhouse Cave and back with enough daylight to be safe, but I take what I can.”* 

The group question Agatha further on recent events in Underton, and she reveals several key pieces of information.

> *“Started with people acting strangely, you know? Like they wasn’t themselves. Strange atmosphere settled in, but none of us saw what was coming next. Folk started disappearing. Vanished, right outta their homes. Screams in the night. That didn’t last long. Soon after that the nightcrawlers came streaming outta the tunnels, ravaged the whole place. It was… a lot of people died. Friends of mine, torn apart. And my Henry snatched up. Been quiet ever since. Save for at night."*

The group ask her more about her husband.

> *“We ran the Schoolhouse, in town. Back when there was a town. We could never have kids of our own. So we moved out West, set up a school here to take care of the children of others. It brought him comfort, I think, and me too.”*

Finally, the conversation turns to the subject of how Agatha survived and how they might make their escape.

> *“Every day I make my way to the roof of the Station House and set a signal fire. I don’t know why any more, there ain’t no help coming. The only Tecs that made it out didn’t come back. I’ve been using the Station House to store things, I’ve fortified it.*
> 
> *Train’s our only way out of here. Only way we’ll get far enough by sundown to outrun the nightcrawlers. Train needs coal and it needs water though, neither of which we have.*
> 
> *I’ve been trying to rebuild a sail for the windmill. Them night-crawlers destroyed it, see. It brings groundwater up from the well for the train and for drinking, and they took it out. They can think, they can strategise, see. They’re not animals, not wholly at least.*
> 
> *We wouldn’t need the windmill necessarily if we had a Quager, they can move the water see. It’s their magecraft. Strange group of ‘em came through here not even a week ago. I saw ‘em go into the mines before the soldier’s arrived. I know I should’ve stopped them but Quagers, you know, I didn’t know what they might do to me.*
> 
> *Even if I fix the windmill and we get water, ain’t no way to get the coal. It’s locked in the coal tower, six-inch thick iron. We could use the key if we could get our hands on it but I’ve searched everywhere I can during the days. It must’ve been on the Chief Engineer. He must’ve had it with him when he was taken, into the mines."*

The group pick up on the fact that Agatha saw the [[The Rogue Marismente|Rogue Marismente]], and ask her about [[Eva Vanderbilt]]. They explain that they can't leave until they find her.

> *“Now that you mention it, there was a small one with ‘em. Much smaller than the others. Went into the mines with the rest. If you knew them, I’m sorry, but ain’t nobody coming out of there alive.”*

[[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] reaches out with his mind to Gwynevere, and feels the familiar spark of connection. Through her eyes, he surveys the town. The darkness of night has descended, and all is silent. Swooping closer however, Gwynevere begins to see several shapes moving between the buildings. They are hulking, standing at least 10 ft tall. They move like birds of prey, legs folding backwards at the knee as they walk. First there are tens, then dozens, then more. Caeldrid returns to the room and relays what he's seen. Agatha looks into the flame of her gas lamp.

> *"They only move at night. They came out of the mines, far as I can tell. I don’t know for sure but I know that’s where they go back to in the day and that’s where they take folks. They’re using the tunnels, see. It’s the light, the sunlight. They hate it. Soon as sun comes up they scatter like rats in a grain house. Only thing that’s kept me alive, that and the birds.”*

Eli stands up to look at the chiropterrings. Inside their cage, as they move about, he sees an object in the corner. It is a safe, with a dial lock numbered one to thirty. He studies again the wall of mail compartments and removes the three letters. Inside each, he finds only a small, hexagonal weight. He notices that each of the compartments A-Z have a matching indent in their base, and there is a 27th compartment which is sealed shut. After some deliberation with the group, Eli places the weights in the compartments for K, E and Y. The locked compartment swings open to reveal a key inside. It unlocks the chiropterring cage. Agatha warns against going inside, and Eli discovers why as the creatures surround him and begin to bite and scratch at him as he attempts to get to the safe. Wild Bill remembers the bone-whistle used by Hermus at Fortuna to soothe the creatures and blows on it. They return to their perches.

Looking for a combination for the safe, the group find a strange letter in one of the drawers of the desk. It is addressed to [[Gideon]], who Agatha confirms was the mail-keeper in Underton.

> *“Dearest Gideon,*
> 
> *We packed up and left on the 28th. After that it was right to the next adventure. All that was left for us was building the courage to go. Sixteen paces east from there should do it. Once that’s done, we can handle anything.*
> 
> *I hope this helps you remember!*
> 
> *From your closest friend”*

Puzzling over this for a while, the Players eventually decipher its meaning and enter the code L28, R2, L4, R16 to unlock the safe. Inside they find a triangular metal object. It bears a partial symbol, indicating it is one part of a greater whole. The group decide to show it to Agatha, but are stopped in their tracks by the sound of a frantic horse neighing outside…
