# The Events of Session 54

[[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] reconciles with [[Gambit]] and [[Wild Bill]] as they recover from their respective possessed states. [[Amotti]] thanks [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] for reasons the group is not entirely clear on. While the rest of the Players recover from the incident with [[Curse of the Vampyr - Book|Curse of the Vampyr]], [[Sel]] takes a closer look at the glass partition separating the emaciated figure strapped to the chair from the rest of the lab. Running his fingers over the surface, he discovers a hidden seam in the shape of a doorway and proposes this might be the entrance to the room beyond.

Despite Sel's protestations, [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] takes out her crowbar and jams the thin end into the seam in the glass. She then tries to force the door open, pushing with great force on the opposite end of the crowbar. The door begins to give, however hairline fractures start to appear in the glass. She looks to the rest of the party for confirmation she should continue.

> Eli - thumbs up
> 
> Caeldrid - thumbs down
> 
> Amotti - thumbs up
> 
> Gambit - thumbs up
> 
> Silas - thumbs down
> 
> Sel - pretends not to see
> 
> Wild Bill - thumbs down

With the vote tied, Lythara continues anyway and the cracks grow larger. Sel, feigning ignorance, instead sends his mage-hand to aid her in pushing the crowbar. This last burst of force cause the glass to shatter in a rough circular pattern around the base of the crowbar. There is a sharp intake of air, as if the room beyond the glass was re-pressurising when opened to the atmosphere of the rest of the lab. Lythara pushes the door open and moves inside.

Lythara approaches the emaciated figure in the chair. Pressing her fingers underneath his chin, she feels for a pulse. There is none. Beside the body, she sees a steel tray-table carrying an array of medical implements such as syringes, forceps and a sharp scalpel. She then checks the straps holding the body to the chair and sees that, while the ones around the ankles and right arm are pulled tight, the strap around the left wrist is slightly frayed and worn. Eli too checks the pulse of the body, this time on its wrist, and again feels nothing.

Leaving the figure behind, the group decide to explore beyond the vault door. Caeldrid moves the steel chair blocking the handle with a harsh scrape of metal on metal. He spins the bar and pulls open the heavy steel door. Beyond, the clinical white light of the lab spills out into a dark tunnel, just like the one they entered from. The Players enter, but Wild Bill intentionally lags slightly behind to ask something of Silas.

> *"Silas, with your uh… ability… do you think you would be able to show me what I saw when I attacked Amotti?"*

> *"I could certainly try, if you were sure. By my own admission, I am not fully in control of it, and it can be quite dangerous."*

> *"All the same. I'll think on it, thank you feller. If you don't mind my asking, someone of your… nature… did you know anything about all this of vampyrs before coming here?"*

> *"Can't say that I did, Bill. I may look like a devil but I try not to dabble in the dark arts too much… if I can help it."*

Silas flashes a sly wink at Lythara, who rolls her eyes. Bill thinks for a moment.

> *"Well ain't that just the thing, here's you looking like a devil, yet it seems I might actually be one."*

Caeldrid and Eli are the first to reach the end of the tunnel. In the light of Caeldrid's sword, the see that they have come to a dead end. The tunnel wall here has collapsed, and broken support struts lie amidst fallen rubble and rocks. Slumped against this cave-in and unmoving, Caeldrid and Eli see a body. He is short and plump, with pointed features. Vacant, beady eyes stare out endlessly from behind horn-rimmed glasses. [[Granville Milton]]. Or what is left of him.

Beside Milton's body, Eli sees an opened briefcase, discarded and face down like a fallen book resting on its pages. He sees that it is surrounded by smashed glass. In one of Milton's hands, he sees an unbroken glass vial with stopper removed. Eli finds another object lying next to Milton's body. A metallic, triangular seal to the Town Hall vault. Unlike the others, this one is chipped and dirtied. Inspecting Milton's hands, Eli sees that beyond the ends of his fingerless gloves his fingertips are worn away and bloodied to the point of exposing bone. With a chill, Eli realises Milton must have been using his seal to try and dig his way out. He likely died of dehydration, if suffocation didn't take him first.

Lythara arrives and stoops to pick up the vial in Milton's hand. She sees that there is a small amount of the shimmering spore-dust remaining within. She seems some residue around the edges of Milton's mouth and realises its lips are curled into a faint smile, as might fall across one's face when sinking into the comfort of their favourite armchair. Grimly, she wonders aloud that he might have chosen to take refuge in visions of the past rather than face the dark truth of his demise. Joining the others, Amotti asks what might have caused the tunnel collapse. Might it have been deliberate? Caeldrid ponders why the Mine Overseer would have been unaware of this. Lythara recalls her vision, and proposes that the dead scientists must have tried to warn Milton before he shot them. Knowing the risks but seeking clarity, the group decide to use some of the [[The Fungal Spores|Fungal Spores]] to try and visualise the events leading to Milton's death. Amotti opens his mouth to voice his opinion and Lythara uses the opportunity to force a dab of the spores into his mouth.

Amotti's vision darkens, growing faint until he can no longer see his companions. Suddenly, he sees a faint blue light, then another. As they come into focus, he sees that they are coming from the cool glow of bioluminescent fungi growing on the walls of the room he now occupies. He looks down, and sees that he is in bed. A soft-down mattress beneath him, and a luxurious quilt on top. A wave of warm comfort washes over him as he looks to the face above him. Kind and gentle, pale lilac-grey skin and soft purple eyes. His mother. She smiles, but then a rumble rises from the ground below and the room shakes briefly. Instinctively, Amotti pulls the quilt up to his chin. His mother speaks.

> *"Don’t be afraid children. You are blood of the House of Xai. If you trust to each other, you have nothing to fear. Now, what say we hear your favourite just one more time?"*

Amotti looks to his left. He sees a small Drow girl, inquisitive eyes and sharp features, staring back at him wearily. She sits upright on the end of her bed, listening intently to her mother while regarding Amotti's fearfulness with disdain. His sister, Maeve. His mother reaches behind her, and lifts a book. The front cover is adorned with a glowing full moon. As she begins to read, her voice fades and Amotti finds himself back in the tunnel, facing Lythara once again. The others ask Amotti what he saw, and he shares his vision with them. Frustrated, they wonder why the spore-dust may have had a different effect this time than when Lythara used it. Eli proposes the effects may simply be unreliable, varying from use to use.

With the Town Hall seal in hand, the group decide to leave the Mining Co. Headquarters and make for the saloon, seeking to find another vault seal before the suns sets. They head back up the tunnel, with Wild Bill and Eli leading the way. Approaching the open vault door, they stop in their tracks.

> "Knock… knock… knock…"

The sound is coming from inside the lab beyond the door. The pair draw their weapons.

"Knock… knock… knock…"

Readying themselves, Wild Bill and Eli jump through the door. All seems still. They look to the area behind the glass partition. The emaciated figure, once strapped to the chair, now stands at the face of the glass. He holds a scalpel in his hand. Unmoved by the Players arrival, from behind the glass he simply bangs his head on the surface, again, again and again.

> "Knock… knock… knock…"

Lythara approaches the glass, and waves her hand in front of the creature. Its hollowed, vacant eyes look up at her. Its head then slowly turns toward the shattered glass and open door to its cell, and it begins to amble toward the entrance - uncaring of the broken glass embedding itself in its feet as it steps. Lythara stands in front of it, and begins to try to reason with the figure, before it slashes toward her with the scalpel. Sel steps forward, flames erupting from an arcane dragon head conjured before him. The flames engulf the creature, and it shuffles forward with skin scorched and bones cracking. With one swing of her glaive, Lythara splits its skull at the cranium. The creature breathes a last sigh, as if relieved, and collapses to the floor. The group look to each other, and silently agree to leave the Mining Co. behind.

Once again outside, the Players see that daylight on this level grows short, and Eli suggests they make straight for the saloon. Silas agrees.

> *"Do you know, that's the first good idea you've had since leaving the Outpost."*

A short way down the road, they come to the face of the wooden saloon building. Ramshackle in its construction, it appears to occupy two floors. A short set of steps lead up to a porch with a wooden balustrade. Above the door, creaking in the stale air, a sign reads: "The Canary's Rest".

Ascending the stairs, the Players push through the door. Sel steps through last.

Beneath his feet, Sel sees fallen leaves. Some the colour of amber. Some the colour of yellow fire. Some the colour of burnt cinder toffee. He looks to his sides and above and sees trees start to appear, colossal Yuirwoods with trunks as wide as towers. Their canopies ascend beyond vision into a dewy fog. Sel follows the path before him. Eventually, he sees a faint plume of chimney smoke rising from up ahead. It is coming from a simple wooden cottage. He approaches, and sees a man standing on its porch, nonchalantly leaning over the balcony and smoking a long, thin wooden pipe.

He is slim and lithe, with wavy brown hair that frames his angular face. It looks somewhat greasy and unwashed. He also has a thin moustache and goatee. He wears a dirtied white shirt open at the collar, and a pair of rough cloth trousers held up by braces. Around his neck, a pair of brass, telescopic goggles  hangs. He puts them to his eyes to better see Sel as he approaches. When he moves, he does so slowly. Deliberately. Sel recognises this man. Zelraun.

As Sel draws nearer, he looks down at his hands. They are soft-skinned and pallid lilac-grey in colour. He looks up, and Zelraun removes his goggles. He smiles, and licks his thumb before using it to put out his pipe and wiping it on his trousers. He looks at Sel with two glinting eyes of granite-grey and a wry smile underneath.

> *"And where have you been hiding? Your notes are up to date, I take it? We're finished now for today in any case. Don't bother preparing supper for me tonight, but I will need you to pack my things. I'll be away for a few weeks."*

Sel moves past him and through the door to the cottage. The man follows and continues to speak, distractedly gathering some things scattered around the room.

> *"I'm off to Candlekeep, they've finally granted my access request. There are answers in that library, somewhere, I'm sure of it. While I'm gone, don't do anything I wouldn't do. And certainly don't do anything that I would."*

Sel nods, and makes his way to a set of stairs he knows from memory are at the back of the room. As he begins to descend, his vision fades and he hears a last word from Zelraun behind him.

> *"Don't worry.. even if someone were to find this place, I've made the incantations and the shield guardian is in place. Besides, I've seen you training, you can handle yourself."*

Meanwhile, the others find themselves inside the saloon.

The saloon doors swing closed behind you. Somewhere in the room beyond, a woman plays piano and softly sings to the crowd. This and the warm, yellow glow of gas lamps welcomes you to [[The Canary's Rest Saloon|The Canary's Rest]]. Looking into the room before you, you see circular tables and stools dotted like islands in a sea of lacquered oak. On them, distracted patrons chatter like birds in a cage, dissecting the day’s labours over a game of dice or a shared bottle of whiskey. Doors at the back of the room swing open and closed to the rhythm of the music, a serving man ferrying plates of simple fare out to the clientele. Following the sound in the air, you see a raised platform to your right where sits a glistening black piano. On a stool in front of it, a woman with hair as black as the polished surface of her instrument plays for the gathered drinkers.

On your left, a smooth wooden bar stretches out in a gentle curve. Empty glasses glint like stars upon its dark sky, catching the light of the lamps. Next to it, a staircase slopes upward to a balcony. A beautiful woman of tumbling red hair and cushioned bust rests a hand on the banister, looking out with a smile to the room below.  Behind the bar, a barman idly polishes a whisky tumbler with a dishcloth and smiles to himself.

![[canarys_rest_description.png]]

The group look to each other, seeking confirmation that they are seeing the same thing. It seems that they are, and they move further into the saloon, wishing to investigate further. Eli and Lythara discusses whether this might too be another vision, brought on by their exposure to the spores. However, as a server passes Lythara, she subtly brushes his shoulder and he turns to apologise.

> *"Pardon me, ma'am. Welcome, please, do take a seat."*

Puzzled by the tangibility of this vision, Lythara approaches a group of miners at a nearby table, who are engaged in a game of dice. As she speaks, one of the miners raises his hand to hush her, shaking a set of dice in a cup in his hand. He throws them onto the table and they bounce before settling. He stands and studies the numbers.

> *"Now why the fuck, would you interrupt a man during a throw…"*

Lythara loses patience and slams her hand on the table. This causes the dice to lift, and they settle on different numbers. There is a groan from some of the miners and the one standing restudies the dice. He looks to Lythara for the first time as a smile grows on his face.

> *"Darling, well you might just be my lucky charm!"*

Lythara ignores this, and questions the man on what he thinks is going on outside, and whether he thinks he is safe here in the saloon.

> *"Well, sounds like you've been talking to my wife. Out there is mining. Mining's work. In here is play. Why, in here's just about the safest place we could be, isn't that right boys?"*

A slurred cheer of agreement goes up from the other miners. Lythara leaves them to their dice.

> *"Now you let me know if you want to join us, y'hear?"*

Caeldrid, wishing to test the boundaries of their new reality, makes his way back to the saloon doors and exits. He finds himself in deep forest. Leaves of murky green cling to oak trees, and a glossy night sky speckled with stars hangs above. He looks around, and eventually sees a faint white light up ahead. He moves forward, and discovers that it is coming from a white owl perched on a high branch. She cocks her head to study him, and then flies off with a forlorn hoot toward a tree further up the path. One step after another, Caeldrid follows her in this manner. Eventually, they come to a silent glade in the forest. The full moon hangs high above, and illuminates the scene below.

Caeldrid sees that, in the centre of the clearing, there is a stone tomb. On it, sits a gleaming set of plate armour. He recognises it as his own, and looks down at his body. It is roughly clothed, with the cloth torn in places and dark red patches where he has sustained wounds. He approaches the tomb and finds a bowl of powdered medicinal root and a pitcher of crystal water. As if from memory, he mixes the water with the root and drinks deep. Placing the bowl back down, he sees a sword and scabbard that has been placed against the tomb. As he looks at it, a white-green glow begins to shine from the blade within its sheath.

The moonlight intensifies. Growing until almost blinding. From above, a pair of ghostly white feet materialise, beneath the beginnings of an ethereal white dress. Caeldrid's vision fades as they descend from the sky…
