# The Events of Session 53

Stumped by the multi-button panel barring their entry to the Geological Survey room, [[Wild Bill]] and [[Gambit]] leave the others to check the Mining Co. headquarters for something they may have missed. In Mine Overseer [[Granville Milton|Milton]]'s office, Wild Bill finds that a document linked to the geological survey bears the Captured Flame symbol of [[The Scientist]]. Finding nothing else of note, he and Gambit once again explore the Miner's Rec Room. Their eyes are drawn to the discarded set of dominos on one of the tables and they decide to collect the set - for future recreation if for nothing else. Wild Bill also finds a simple charcoal drawing of a curly-haired women - presumably one of the former miner's sweethearts. Before returning to the others, Bill checks in on Rusty Jack , whom he has tied up outside the building. Noticing that his companion is nervously pacing and whinnying, he decides to bring him inside. While untying Jack, the hairs on Bill's neck stand up and he turns. He sees only the elongating shadows of the buildings around him and the gentle whistle of the stale underground wind through the bars of the porch.

Wild Bill and Gambit return to the others, feeling themselves no closer to solving the door code. Gambit shows [[Amotti]] one of the dominos and Amotti holds it up in front of him. [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] looks from the domino to the door panel and something twigs in his mind. Sensing this realisation, [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] steps forward and enters '345' in each section not numerically, but to mimic the representation of those numbers as they would appear on the face of a domino.

![[underton_mining_co_door_code_solution.png]]

The door clicks open, and Eli pulls on the handle to open up the entrance. The Players move inside and find themselves in what looks like a laboratory. There are rows of wooden benches and stools, and the work surfaces are littered with samples of rock and coal. There are various instruments for determining composition and quality of the geological samples and beakers filled with unknown solutions. The walls of the room are lined with steel bookcases at regular intervals and decorated with carved iron symbols of the Captured Flame.

Something catches [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]]'s eye, and she moves to one of the bookcases at the far side of the room. She kneels and picks up a broken glass sample vial from the floor. Studying it, she sees that inside is a strange, powdery residue. It shimmers and seems to change colour depending on how the light hits it. Overcome by curiosity, she dabs some of the substance with her finger and touches it to her tongue.

Meanwhile, Eli notices something else. On the floor in front of the bookcase where Lythara is standing, he sees scrape marks on the floor that curve outward and back into the room. He realises that the bookcase must have been moved recently, and perhaps hides some hidden entrance to another room. He turns to tell Lythara and stops in his tracks. Her eyes have glazed over white, and she stands motionless and unresponsive.

Lythara looks around. She is in the same room, however her companions are nowhere to be seen. Her hearing is muffled and her vision hazy. She hears a faint noise behind her, and turns. She sees the door to the room open, and a figure steps through. He is a short, rotund man with a mole-like face and pointed features. Beady black eyes dart from side to side behind horn-rimmed spectacles. He wears a roughly cut pinstripe suit, too short at the extremities and fingerless gloves of dirty cloth. He looks around the room nervously as he clutches a briefcase to his chest. Lythara recognises him from Caeldrid's pictogram as Mine Overseer Milton.

Lythara tries to call out to him and stands in front to block his path. Milton doesn't react to her presence. In fact, he moves right through her. She watches as he makes his way over to one of the Captured Flame sculptures on the wall and reaches up to turn it upside down. As he does, the bookcase at the far end of the room swings out into the room revealing the entrance to a hidden tunnel. Milton enters it and Lythara's vision ends.

Lythara snaps back to consciousness to see Eli standing before her, confused and alarmed. She explains to the group what she saw, and they propose it might have been a vision of the recent past. Lythara walks up to the same Captured Flame sculpture she saw Milton turn, and does the same. It rotates freely, and the hidden entrance behind the bookcase is revealed.

Bracing themselves, the group make their way into the tunnel beyond. It has a packed-earth floor and walls of rock and mud, supported by regularly-spaced struts. The bookcase closes behind them, and Gambit illuminates the dark with his glowing bolts to the amazement of Silas. He then casts a magical trap on the entrance, lying in wait should someone try to follow the group. For a moment, the group again wonder who might be watching them, and Eli suggests that it might be Red - after all he was watching them from afar already back in Clement's Hollow. Amotti sees that the entrance can also be opened from this side by turning a similar sculpture set into the rocky wall of the tunnel. Reassured, the party push on. Lingering behind for a moment with a strange urge, Gambit pulls out Curse of the Vampyr. He studies it with a strange fascination, noticing that it is somehow heavier in his hands than before, and stops a moment to gaze at the glowing red jewel on its cover.

Eli sees a small slot of light at the end of the passageway. He approaches, and comes to a steel door secured by rivets. The light is coming from a letter-box sized glass panel set just above its centre. Looking through, he sees a room very similar in nature to the one they just left, but different. Like the Geological Survey Room, the space beyond the door appears to be a laboratory. However, the benches and stools here are made of steel, and the floor is clean white marble. The entire room is lit by an unnaturally bright and clinical light, which Eli sees is coming from strips set in the ceiling - a technology he hasn't yet encountered. At the back of the room, he sees a large, circular vault door with a spinning bar handle. The handle has been blocked by a propped steel chair. Beneath the chair, lies a body face-down and wearing a long white lab coat. Its hand is outstretched toward the chair, and Eli notices a pool of blood at the centre of its back.

Cautiously, the Players enter the room. Looking to their right, they see another figure in a white lab coat, slumped against the right hand wall and head lolling onto the chest. In the centre of this figures chest is a bullet wound, marked red with dried blood. Eli kneels to inspect the body, and deduces that death must have occurred at least three days ago. On the left of the room is an even more chilling sight. This side of the lab is blocked off by what seems to be a seamless glass partition. Behind it, there is a steel chair, set at an angle facing upward and fitted with leather straps. In this chair, sits another body. Stripped of all clothing and secured to the chair, its hollowed eyes and gaping mouth stare vacantly upward. Its skin, darkened and stretched, is pulled over a bony and emaciated frame.

Almost without thinking, Lythara once again dabs some of the powdery residue onto her tongue, hoping to reveal something of what transpired in this room prior to their arrival. She is once again snapped into a vision of the past. She sees Milton enter the room. In muffled tones, he says something unintelligible to the two scientists who stand at the benches. They look to each other worriedly, as Milton pulls a revolver from his suit jacket. Hands shaking, he points it at each scientist in turn, with both men raising their hands in surrender. Milton shoots. First, the one on the right. A bullet through the chest. Next, the one on the left, a bullet through the back as he tries to flee. Lythara watches one as Milton unlocks the vault door and moves beyond it. As the vision begins to fade, the scientist closest to her crawls toward the door with his last ounce of strength. He pushes the vault shut and bars the handle with a steel chair, before slumping onto his face. Behind the glass, the figure strapped to the chair mouths a silent scream, and writhes from side to side in agony.

Lythara snaps back to reality, and explains her revelations to the group. They hypothesise that she may have witnessed Milton trying to flee the town on the day [[The Nightcrawlers]] attacked, potentially taking his seal with him. Looking for further information, Eli finds a lab book on one of the benches. Although unfamiliar with the terms and format used within, he is able to glean a few details. The scientists here were studying the effects of refined [[The Fungal Spores|Fungal Spores]] on human test subjects. They note that the spores seem to allow one to experience visions of the past. Not only the past of the individual exposed, but that of the fungus itself. Hypotheses are made that, as some mushrooms represent only the individual sprouting appendages of a much larger single organism, so exposure to the spores may allow one access to a collective memory. To commune briefly with a mind much larger and older than their own. Of the current test subject, the notebook makes a chilling conclusion. Repeated exposure causes extreme muscle deterioration and emaciation, but not death. The subject's life is drawn out as their strength is sapped, becoming hollowed and empty.

Eli looks toward Lythara with concern, then notices something else. Gambit is not with them. With a sudden panic, Amotti rushes back up the tunnel to find his friend. He sees Gambit sat on Ralf's back, vacant and unresponsive. He is holding [[Curse of the Vampyr - Book|Curse of the Vampyr]]. Using his mage hand, Amotti tries to grab the book, but Gambit's grip tightens. He resorts to magical command, and enchants Gambit to hand over the book. He does so, and Amotti pulls it toward him with his mage hand. He sees that something is very wrong. A drip, drip, drip echoes in the tunnels as the book comes closer to him. In full view, he can see that its pages are swollen and engorged with blood, and it drips onto the floor below. He looks in fear at Gambit, whose skin has turned a pallid grey and whose eyes have sunken into their sockets in exhaustion.

Amotti picks Gambit up and carries him back to the others. He tries to heal his friend. The others watch as an arcane moon appears over Gambit, which then cracks like an egg, depositing thousands of tiny spiders onto his body which set to the work of healing him. Some colour returns to Gambit's cheeks, but plateaus and begins to fade again. Wild Bill takes the book and studies it in his hands. His eyes glaze over at the sight of the rich, fatty blood within. Gambit snaps to attention. Like a wild animal, he leaps at Bill in a fit of jealous rage and tries to claw at him to recover the book. Unable to do so, he draws his short sword and begins to slash at Bill - who barely reacts. Caeldrid shouts to Lythara.

> *"DESTROY IT!"*

In one swift motion, Lythara swings her glaive in an upward arc and slices cleanly through the spine of Curse of the Vampyr. To the Player's horror, where the cut was made several sinewy strands of dripping red blood spring forth like a spider's web and pull the two halves back together. The book begins to vibrate violently and emit a piercing shriek as its central jewel glows ever brighter and brighter. Caeldrid grabs it from the air and runs into the tunnel. Lythara follows him. Facing each other, Caeldrid drops the book onto the floor. In a flash, Lythara lets loose a searing firebolt, which engulfs the pages in scarlet flame. The shrieking intensifies, and the book levitates into the air as it begins to boil and its form ripples. With a final stroke, Caeldrid light his blade in blinding white flame, repelling the darkness around him. He brings its point down swiftly and forcefully directly through the jewel on the book's cover, pinning it to the earthen ground below. The book writhes and squirms before exploding in a cloud of smouldering ash. Caeldrid, sighing and catching his breath, looks at Gambit - who looks back with a regained flicker of recognition.
