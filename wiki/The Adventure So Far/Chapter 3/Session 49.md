# The Events of Session 49

[[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] slides the corpse of the [[The Protectorate|Protectorate]] soldier off of his sword and struggles to his feet. Above, the others pull the remaining chains and release the last shafts of light from the roof of the Assembly Hall onto the mural below. [[Gambit]] has Ralf clear the pews to either side of the room, and the Players see that the remaining two symbols on the codex dial are tree and catfish. [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] enters the final code and the glass cabinet opens, allowing him to retrieve the decorative weight for the grandfather clock.

The Players, eager to retrieve the Sun Key, leave the Assembly Hall without further exploration. With Caeldrid exhausted, they again choose first to rest in the Council Chamber again, losing sunlight with every delay. Afterward, they make their way upstairs to the grandfather clock and Eli places the weight in its body. This causes the weight to gently swing to either side in a metronomic rhythm, and the hands on the face begin to turn. When both strike six, the crystal clockface pops open and Eli plucks the Sun Key from inside.

They take the Sun Key to the Lower Archive door, and find that it fits, however the door is jammed by some unseen obstacle and will not budge when pushed. Instead, they try the Upper Archive door and find that they can now open it and enter.

Entering, they find themselves on the upper floor of a huge room lined with floor to ceiling bookshelves and filing cabinets. The room here is lit by a gaping hole in the stone ceiling, one that looks to have been the result of a great force, crumbling the stone into the room below. A perimeter walkway stretches off to either side of them, serving walls lined with shelves and old, dusty texts and ledgers bound in leather. At the end of the walkway to their left, there are stairs leading to the lower floor.

From the lower floor, the cloying stench of damp and rot rises. The smell of wood, paper and flesh submerged in stagnant water and left to fester. Peering over the railing in front of them, they look down on a scene which steals the breath and turns the stomach for a moment. The lower floor of the room is flooded. In the murky water, the bodies of the dead drift as if suspended in their final moments. Protectorate soldiers, some face down, some eyes staring terribly ahead. Some have been dismembered. Among them, they also see the bodies of some of the creatures which presumably besieged them, wings sodden in the pooled water and clouds of black blood billowing from beneath their broken bodies.

Against one of the lower floor walls, by its orientation presumably the wall which houses the door back out to the West Wing balcony, there is a stack of corpses. Protectorate bodies piled high over each other, twisted and contorted in the fear and desperation of trying to escape whatever fate laid waste to them. [[Silas Seer|Silas]] winces and lets out a gasp, clutching his head and screwing up his eyes as he sinks to his knees.

> *"I can hear them screaming…"*

Splitting up briefly, the group search the Upper Archive shelves for any books of interest. Caeldrid finds what he discovers is a town census, detailing the names, familial connections, occupations and residences of some of the [[Underton]] populace. Set into one of the Lower Archive shelves, Eli spies a gold-plated lectern set into an alcove against the far wall. He sees no choice but to explore the murky flooded floor below. Cautious of the dead in the water, Eli strips to his small clothes and places a dagger between his teeth. He then wades through the chest-high water toward the lectern. In the pool, he notices a body quite unlike the others.

One body amongst the others stands out, floating toward you still and lifeless. Neither Protectorate soldier or monster, as you look closer you recognise the nature of this creature. Alone amongst the dead, but drifting alongside them all the same, is one of the [[Marismente]]. A male. His kelp-like hair sticks to his sallow grey-green cheeks. Bulbous, milky-white eyes look forever upward. There is dried blue-green blood below his nostrils and around his eyes, as if weeping. On his body, he wore armour woven of tough bark and sea-grass. Examining further, you see several deep gashes and tears in his leathery skin where the claws and stingers of several monstrous foes have left their mark.

Eli wonders if this figure might be the one responsible for flooding the room, and recalls Silas' tale of the Marismente the brothers at Fortuna intercepted having strange powers beyond that of the average member of their race. Unable to search the body thoroughly in the water, he pushes it to Caeldrid waiting at the bottom of the stairs, who finds a Bottle of Sunlight hooked to a belt around its waist.

Reaching the lectern, Eli finds upon it a small marble statue of a closed book. He collects it, placing it under his hat, and turns to make his way back to the group. From the corner of his eye, he sees movement. At first, just a ripple. Then, the curve of a sleek, bottle-green back just breaching the water before re-submerging. A shiver runs down his spine, and he calls to Caeldrid and Gambit. They see nothing. Eli climbs the shelves of the Lower floor, suspending himself above the water. Again he searches for the creature, nearly losing his grip on the damp and slimy wood in the process. He sees snaking ripples through the still surface as some deep menace searches in the murk below.

From above, Ralf lets down a rope from the balcony, which dangles in front of Eli just out of reach. He decides to jump. Leaping, Eli manages to grasp on to the rope and swings out above the death pool beneath him. The weight of the marble book beneath his hat causes it to slide off of his head, and he instinctively reaches out a hand to grab it. As he does, the creature rises from below. Sleek, slippery flesh glinting in the light. Lidless white eyes dead and staring. It snaps its jaws of long, needle-like teeth jutting out at all angles shut and misses Eli's hand by a millimetre. The others see that it is large enough to swallow him in two bites. Panic setting in, Eli cries to Ralf to pull up the rope, which he does and Eli flops onto the Upper balcony. Panting and regaining his breath, he reveals the book to the others.

Noting that they still need to find the Moon Key, the Watchful Sword and the Offered Chalice, the group decide to leave the Archive as quickly as possible. Before doing so however, Eli manages to tag the creature below with a crossbow bolt, eliciting a piercing hiss from the monster as it dives below once again and does not re-emerge. The Players then take the book to the Ceremony Room, and place it in the hands of the statue of the Scholar. In doing so, they cause the book to pop open and unfold, revealing the Moon Key in an inset inside.

With all three keys in hand, the Players make their way back to the fireplace in the Council Chamber. Noting which key is which (Caeldrid with the Star Key, Eli with the Sun Key and Gambit with the Moon key) they snap off the symbols from the top and place them in the fireplace. At first, nothing happens. Then, as if blown by a sudden gust of wind, the fire within goes out. The Players hear a scraping of stone on stone as a hidden doorway opens in the mantle. They find it leads to a narrow crawlspace, too small for Sel or Ralf to enter and small enough that Caeldrid and Lythara's armour scrapes the walls as they pass through.

In the crawlspace, Eli notes that ascending steps seem to be taking them to the Upper Floor. Gambit illuminates the area with some shining nuts and bolts, revealing cobwebs and dust in the gloom. Eli studies the walls, and sees that the dust has recently been disturbed. At the top of the stairs, the group reach a dead end and a landing. Eli pushes the wall in front of him, and finds a hidden exit.

Pushing the panel in front of you, it swings open with a creak. Stepping into the room beyond, you see that it is a grand office. Turning, you see that you have entered via a hidden cavity behind a huge framed portrait. It shows a pompous looking figure, plump and red-faced, in ceremonial mayoral robes and jewellery. His expression is one of faux grandeur.

Looking around the office itself, you see that the walls are lined with bookcases and filing cabinets. The centre of the room is dominated by a large wooden desk, behind which sits an empty, plush leather chair. There seems to be only one other entry point, a sizeable door made of iron which appears to be locked from the inside by a sliding bar. 

The Players realise that they have entered the room behind the large iron door on the Upper Balcony. Eli moves toward it, and sees a body on the ground. It is that of a plump, balding man in fine clothes. His hand is outstretched toward the door, and there is a gaping wound on his head. Beside him, a revolver sits in a dark patch of blood on the rug. Eli kneels to search the body, and finds that something is terribly wrong. On the back, nascent wings poke out beneath the waistcoat. The knees have snapped backward at the joint. The outstretched hand ends in sticky green claws. Turning the body, Eli sees that the face is malformed. The eyes are now bulbous and insect-like, and emerging mandibles protrude from the mouth. Forcing himself to search further, he finds a weighty coin purse containing ninety gold pieces and a small leather-bound journal. He opens it and begins to read.
