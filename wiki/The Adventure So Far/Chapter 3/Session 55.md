# The Events of Session 55

Thus the seeds of destiny fall from their pods, blown on the ocean wind to whatever awaits them.

[[Elias Blackwood|Eli]], [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]], [[Amotti]] and [[Wild Bill]] decide to speak further with the staff and clientele of [[The Canary's Rest Saloon|The Canary's Rest]]. Strangely, [[Sel]] and [[Gambit]] are nowhere to be seen. Listening to the sweet music in the air, Wild Bill finds a tear rolling down his cheek. It sounds, to him, just like something his mother used to sing. Lythara studies her surroundings. She notices that, focusing on any one object or point, things come into focus and all seems well. However, when one moves their gaze or turns too quickly, the room seems to shimmer in the yellow-orange lamp light. She tries to pinpoint how this feels, and finds her mind drawn back to the temporal distortion she experienced at [[Fortuna Outpost]].

She and Amotti head over to the bar. The barkeep, idly polishing a whisky tumbler and smiling to himself, looks up as they approach. He is an older man, slick grey hair swept back over his head save for a single stray lock resting on his forehead. He wears a simple white shirt and black waistcoat. His arms and exposed chest are covered in various tattoos depicting Sirens of ancient myth. He greets Lythara and Amotti.

> *"Evening friend, lady. I never known a more beautiful or more tempting song than that of the saloon piano, good liquor and a woman's company - and I for one would be happy to drown in any one of 'em, wouldn't you agree? What can I get you?"*

Lythara, states that she isn't drinking.

> *"Who owns this saloon, where can we find them?"*

The man smiles.

> *"Friends, tonight is about drinking and forgetting. Enjoy yourselves, and try not to cause too much trouble. As for the owner, that would be yours truly, I'm [[Dion]]."*

Lythara picks up on something he said.

> *"What do you mean, 'tonight is for forgetting'?"*

> *"Well miss, I'm a barkeep. Forgetting is what I'm selling."*

Amotti enthusiastically states that he would like a whisky, and Dion begins to pour. As he does, for a moment the whisky pours onto the table instead of the tumbler below. Lythara sees Dion's hand begin to shake, and a brief look of terror crosses his face. He shakes his head, and notices the spilled whisky, as if for the first time.

> *"My, I am sorry. Losing my touch…"*

He cleans up the spill with the cloth hung over his shoulder. Lythara asks him, what day it is.

> *"Why, you two must've breezed right in on the wind. Look around you. It's Highharvestide, 1st of Marpenoth. Please, enjoy the celebrations."*

Lythara's mind goes back to their arrival in [[Underton]]. A litter of massacred [[The Protectorate|Protectorate]] on the ground. A hanging banner, flapping the in the stale wind above them. *Happy Highharvestide.*

Amotti slams the whisky and asks for another. He gets into a disagreement with Dion over payment, promising that he and his friends are good for it should Dion set up a tab for them. Refusing without a suitable deposit, Amotti then magically commands Dion to give him the whisky. Failing to resist the spell, he does so. Amotti and Lythara leave the bar and go to regroup with the others.

Meanwhile, Wild Bill approaches the woman at the piano. She is elegant, beautiful, and clad in a flowing dress of deep-indigo. Her fingers deftly caress the keys, and her soft voice fills the air with song.

*(Music: [PAN'S LABYRITH LULLABY LONG LONG TIME AGO - ONE HOUR THEME - THE SOUL OF LABYRINTH](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=XUOhZ-lVKoc&si=DC1zGnla9GwTXJ0E))*


Wild Bill stands to the side of her piano and leans an elbow. The songstress looks up at him, her face porcelain and her eyes two deep pools of liquid sapphire. Bill introduces himself.

> *"Miss, if you don't mind my saying. That is a beautiful song. Why such a sad number on a night like this?"*

> *"This tune? Oh you know it don't you? Sure, everyone does. It's Dion's favourite. I’m the only one he’ll let near the piano, you know. I think my mother used to sing it to me. I wouldn't call it sad, now."*

A single tear rolls down her cheek.

> *"Seems your eyes disagree."*

> *"Oh (she wipes the tear), I am sorry. Must be the lights. What can I call you?"*

> *"Bill, ma'am."*

> *"I'm [[Mirabelle]]. What have you come looking for here, Bill?"*

> *"Well ma'am, I suppose the truth."*

> *"Truth, is it? Seems there are plenty of folks come see Dion looking for that, these days."*

Wild Bill bids a quiet farewell with the tip of his hat, and rejoins the others. He bumps into the others. Lythara tells them about the incident with Dion, and that she suspects something to be very wrong. Her, Eli and Bill agree to leave and regroup with Gambit and [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] outside. Amotti goes to voice his desire to stay a while, when two large and rough hands appear on his shoulders.

---

Gambit awakes in a dark cavern. He sits in a simple wooden chair. In front of him, a single lamp illuminates a large notice board, pinned with various diagrams and letters. He is in excruciating pain. He looks down to his right, and sees that his right arm has been severed. Where it was, there is now a bloody stump cut off just below the shoulder and wrapped in a red-soaked bandage. From the nearby shadows, a wizened gnome of pointed chin and wispy grey hair appears. Gambit's former mentor, [[Yuna]]. He approaches Gambit while restlessly flexing fingers adorned with rings which, upon closer inspection, are actually nuts and bolts from various pieces of machinery. He carries a bottle of

cleansing alcohol in his hand.

> *"Here, Tidus, quickly. This is no ordinary wound, it must be cleansed."*

He removes the dirty dressings around Gambit's arm and cleanses the stump. Gambit recoils as a sharp, searing pain rises all the way to his head.

> *"Sit still. Stretch out what's left of your arm."*

He places a bandage around the stump. and attaches it to a mechanical wind. It automatically wraps around the wound tight and stops the bleeding. Satisfied, Yuna retreats to some shelves and talks to Gambit while we looks for something.

> *"Tell me, boy, what in the Nine Hells happened out there at [[Greed]]'s mansion? Where is [[Edward]]?"*

Gambit, struggling for words begins to explain how the heist went wrong. How Edward was killed. How, fatefully, Gambit tried to use the object that they were there to retrieve to try and save his brother. He names it, the [[The Dodecahedron|Soulstone]]. A darkly contemplative look falls over his old master's face.

> *"Admirable, but foolish. Only a great mage could use that cursed thing safely. I'm truly sorry about your brother, Tidus, but we must get you safe now. They will be looking for you."*

Gambit acts confused, and asks Yuna why someone would be looking for him. Yuna grows more nervous and impatient.

> *"Don't try to trick me, boy, you know as well as I do what this means. You've used the stone. Messily, but you're alive. That means they need you dead. I heard from the others. It's already been delivered to our connection in Waterdeep. [[The Crimson Syndicate]] should not be underestimated, Tidus, they will hunt you to the ends of the earth. Not to mention Greed himself. He will stop at nothing to find you."*

Yuna finally finds what he is looking for and returns. He looks at Gambit and tears begin to form in his eyes.

> *"Here is what you must do, my dear boy. Forget the past. Forget me. Forget Edward. Run. Run as fast and as far as you can. They will come here first. It’s too late for me, now. But I can do something. I can help you. (He pauses to hold back tears) Raising you and your brother has been the privilege of this old gnome's life. Now… over there, look at the board. Those are all the contracts we have. Any one of them will grant you passage out of here. Listen to me carefully. I must have plausible deniability when they get here. Do you understand? Go, now. Take a contract at random, make sure I can't see which one. Go where it says and get far away, as quickly as you can. Here."*

Yuna hands Gambit a mechanical arm from his hands. It slots in place automatically and closes his wound. He and Gambit tightly embrace.

> *"My last gift to you. I'll turn around now. You run and don't look back. (He cups his hands around Gambit’s face) Farewell, my boy, and may the protection of the Vanguard be with you always."*

Gambit runs and grabs the contract, illuminated on the board.

As his vision fades, he looks at top. It reads:

*"Vanderbilt - Alcadeña"*

Caeldrid comes to, and finds himself once again in the dusty street outside of The Canary's Rest. He pats himself down and looks around. Back to reality, at last. He hears a scuffle behind him. To his surprise, Amotti is thrown through the saloon doors and lands on the porch in front of them. He stands, an indignant look on his face. Caeldrid, asks him what happened, and Amotti begins to rant about how he was unfairly thrown out of the saloon. Caeldrid hushes him.

> *"Wait… the music has stopped."*

Something catches Amotti's eye. On a swinging seat on the porch, Gambit sits with his head jerked back and eyes glazed over white. Amotti gives him a shake, and he comes to with a start. He makes to run, before seeing Amotti in front of him.

With Gambit lucid once again, the trio discuss their respective visions of the past. Gambit casts Detect Magic, hoping to glean some further information about what could be causing these. He detects Dunamancy, the same school of magic he detected in Fortuna Outpost. Amotti looks back to the saloon doors.

> *"I don't know what kind of trance or illusion we are under but maybe, if we find out how it started, we can finish it."*

He, Caeldrid and Gambit ascend the steps once more, looking to retrieve their friends. To their surprise, Wild Bill, Lythara and Eli emerge from the doors unprompted. In turn, each of their heads jerk back and their eyes cloud over.

---

Wild Bill awakens in a bar. Around him, the raucous noise of laughter and heated conversation echoes around the busy room. By his hand, there is a bottle of whisky and a full tumbler. In front of him, a hooded and cloaked figure slumps onto the bar, face down and arms crossed. Bill slaps him on the back and he awakes, clearly intoxicated.

> *"Ah, yes… where was I?"*

The light hits the figure's face and Bill sees that it is an older male Drow of pale lilac-grey skin, handsome but slightly gaunt and stretched. He picks up Bill's whisky and slams it back. As he does so, his cloak falls open slightly, and Bill spies a rolled up piece of parchment sealed with wax and ribbon tantalisingly poking out from a pocket within.

> *"Thank you, my friend. Might your coin stretch to another?"*

Bill obliges and pours another drink, which the Drow greedily swallows.

> *"Now, feller, what say we take a bit of the night air - see if we can't clear our heads a little?"*

> *"Ah yes, yes of course. Lead the way."*

The Drow goes to move from his stool and falls. Bill catches him under the arm and leads him outside. The street is quiet. Light spills from the steamy windows of the bar. In either direction, Bill sees the cobbled street stretch away - lit by regularly-spaced streetlamps around which a coiling fog wraps and shifts in the night air. His drinking companion begins to ramble.

> *"Now hic tell me, friend… what do you know of (he looks from side to side and lowers his voice to a whisper)… adventure?"*

> *"Well plenty, partner, just plenty."*

> *"I knew it, I have a **hic** nose for this sort of thing. Well, I happen to have received an invitation to a rather secretive and lucrative adventure. We could use men of your fighting spirit."*

> *"Is that so?"*

> *"Indeed. Now if you'll excuse me, the call of nature beckons me."*

The Drow moves past Bill and his foot catches on the cobblestones. He stumbles into Bill and, seeing his opportunity, Bill slips the rolled up parchment from his cloak. He is caught in the process.

> *"What are you doing with that contract, friend?"*

> *"Ah well see, feller, I'm just keeping it safe for you."*

> *"Ah yes, of course. How silly of me."*

The Drow disappears around the side of the tavern, and Bill mounts the nearby Rusty Jack. As he speeds off into the night, hoofbeats resonating in the empty streets, he hears the distressed cries of his mark ([[The Drunkard]]) echoing behind him.

---

Lythara walks the halls of a familiar stronghold. To her left and right, dark cells locked by bar and gate line the walls. There is an occasional flash of purple light as an occupant tries to ram themselves against the bars and is thrown back by psionic energy. Ahead of her, tall and stern with deliberate postures and gait, walks a high elf of long grey hair and pointed ears. [[Eldrin]], her commander. He is mid-conversation as Lythara's hearing comes into focus.

> *“We are yet to even scratch the surface of our Order’s power, Lythara. Yours will be the will that enforces it.”*

Lythara says nothing, and silently follows Eldrin to an open cell. Screams resonate from within and light spills into the corridor. In the silhouettes on the floor, Lythara sees a multi-limbed creature writhing in pain.

Eldrin beckons her inside, and she obeys. She finds an unspeakable creature, long spider-like limbs but mammalian fur and features, strapped to a chair. Beside it, a high elf in glistening purple armour telekinetically pushes thin daggers of psionic energy into different points in the creatures body as it recoils in agony. As he pushes the latest dagger into the creature’s spine, it groans as another limb bursts from its chest. Eldrin looks to Lythara.

> *“So now you see. We are nearing control over the evolution of life itself, Lythara. This was your mother’s will, the power she sought her whole life, and we need warriors to wield it.”*

Lythara shakes her head.

> *“No, Eldrin. This has gone too far. It’s wrong, you know it as well as I do.”*

> *“Careful, soldier, you know the consequence to refusal of your commander’s will.”*

Lythara glances to the corner of the room. A bucket with grim contents sits against the wall. In one swift movement, Lythara uses her telekinesis to throw it at Eldrin’s face. He throws a hand up and the bucket is repelled by a psionic shield, but he winces just long enough for Lythara to run past him and out of the cell door.

As she runs, Lythara swings her glaive and breaks the locks of the cells on either side of her. She hears a scream and turns back. The high elf in purple armour, pursuing her down the corridor, has been stopped in his tracks. Unspeakable creatures emerge from the dark of the cells and close in on him. Lythara sees one last look of helpless terror on his face before they pounce. In the frenzy, she sees the elf’s limbs being torn from his body and scattered in the air. As she keeps running, she hears one last warning shouted by Eldrin.

> *“This will be the last mistake you ever make!”*

Eli stands in a dark alleyway, furtively looking out from the shadows to the street beyond. In front of him, light emanates from the steamed-up windows of a tavern. The muffled sound of raucous voices comes from within. He looks up and down the street to either side of the alley. Empty. Fog swirls around black street lamps, their pale white light fighting against its encroachment. Eli waits.

A man emerges from the bar, stumbling on his feet with inebriation. He leans against a nearby building and begins to relieve himself. Eli approaches like a vengeful shadow, silent and swift, and places a dagger to the man’s throat. He begins to laugh.

> *“Ha ha ha… you shouldn’t have come, Blackwood.”*

Words for in Eli’s mouth, as if from memory.

> *“Where is [[Amara]]?”*

The man continues to snicker, and presses his neck a little closer to Eli’s blade.

> *“You think we’re afraid of you, Elias? You’re chasing shadows. You’ll never find her. I tell you what. You let me go, I might even tell you where the drop off is going to be tonight. Who knows? You might even get there in time to stop it.”*

Eli consider this, and his dagger loosens just a millimetre from the man’s throat. From in front of him, emerging from the dark, another man appears. He wields a dagger. Eli runs through his options in his head. He grips his captive’s hair and pulls his head back, backing into the alley as the other man approaches. His hostage curls his lips into a smile.

> *“Say, how is that pretty lady of yours? No, don’t tell me… [[Isabella]], right? Oh yeah, the Crimson Syndicate knows all about her. Now you have to ask yourself, is coming after us worth what it could cost you?”*

Eli hears a noise from behind him and turns his head. A third man steps out from the alley into the street light and pulls a sword from his hip. The first man peels away from Eli as he loosens his grip.

> *“We’ve got people everywhere, you see? Ever-y-where.”*

Eli acts quickly. He slashes the man under his chin with his dagger, causing him to stumble. He pushes him into the second man and runs. Scanning the buildings and streets he knows so well for an escape route, Eli climbs a nearby storage shed. Just as he is about to pull himself up, a hand grips his ankle. Then a second on his other ankle. He is yanked back down to the cobbled street.

Dazed, he looks up as the three men tower over him. The one he slashed holds his chin and looks him straight in the eye.

> *“It’s like I told you, Blackwood. You shouldn’t have come.”*

Eli feels a brutal kick to his temple and his vision goes black.

---

Eli awakens with a jolt on the porch of the Canary’s Rest. Beside him, Wild Bill and Lythara come back from their visions as Caeldrid, Gambit and Amotti look on.

Reunited, the Players discuss their next move. With Sel and Silas still inside the saloon, they agree to go back in to retrieve them. Without entering, they look inside. In the dark interior, they see dust swirling in shimmering patterns. Gambit retrieves some bolts from inside Ralf’s chest cavity. He sparks them with arcane light and tosses them through the saloon doors. The illuminated bolts clank ominously on the floor of the saloon beyond the swinging doors. Without entering, the Players peer into the faint light as their eyes adjust.

Inside, they see several tables like islands in a sea of lacquered oak. At each of them, dead patrons hold empty drinks. Dice go unrolled. A skeleton lies motionless across the curving wooden bar, jaw hanging open and bony fingers gripping a dusty whisky bottle. At the piano, another skeleton lies still. Fingers still on the keys. Skull resting on the glossy black surface. A skeleton garbed in the tattered and shredded remains of a flowing dress of deep, indigo blue.
