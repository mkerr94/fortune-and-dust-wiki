# The Events of Session 47

In the Council Chamber, [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] kneels by the motionless body of the young woman and retrieves the pendant chain from her neck. Hanging from it, he finds a key which has a star symbol at its base. Examining the residue [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] found from the smashed [[Bottle of Sunlight]], Gambit fails to identify its ingredients or figure out how to successfully reproduce it. The Players then discuss the body of the young woman, and theorise that she may have been used as some kind of parasitic host for the reproductive cycle of [[The Nightcrawlers]].

The group then decide to examine the rest of the Council Chamber. [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] looks carefully at the fireplace, and finds that the mantlepiece is adorned with three indentations: one sun, one moon and one star. Calling Eli over, they find that the star decoration atop the key Eli found fits the corresponding slot on the mantlepiece, but notice that it will have to be removed from the key itself before inserting. They note this and decide to hold on to the star key for now.

Deciding to first try and explore the lower floors of the [[Underton Town Hall]], the group make their way back across the Main Hall to investigate the door to the East Wing. They find it too has been sealed by an iron shutter, this time intact. They struggle and fail to raise it by force, noting that there must be a release mechanism somewhere but not finding one in the immediate vicinity of the door. Instead, they return to the West Wing Corridor and try to ascend the stairs at the right end.

Wading through the shallow water that has pooled in the corridor, they reach the bottom of the stairs. They see that the stairs have been blocked off by a makeshift barricade of barrels and broken furniture. Lythara approaches and investigates the sprawled body of a [[The Protectorate|Protectorate]] soldier lying over the barricade. She gently lifts his shoulder to check for useful items, but is taken by surprise when the corpse moves and sinks its teeth into her arm. Lythara stumbles back and the group see two other corpses rise and stumble over the barricade toward them.

The Players engage [[the reanimated]] soldiers, finding their attacks senseless and wild. They seem unphased by physical blows from the group's weapons, but recoil when Caeldrid wields his flaming sword and [[Sel]] rings out his Toll of the Dead. Caeldrid rushes up the steps to confront the corpse grappling with Lythara, but another reanimated soldier crashes into them and sends them careening down the staircase. The group struggle against the risen dead, which begin to attack by spewing corrosive bile over the Players. Eli manages to incapacitate one by plunging his rapier into its gaping mouth, while [[Gambit]] destroys another by blasting through its skull with his revolver.

Lythara, regrouping after forcing off the creature grappling with her, swings her glaive and severs the corpses arms from their sockets. As it stumbles aimlessly, Sel chimes his Toll of the Dead, which causes the reanimated soldier to vibrate violently before exploding in a shower of rotten flesh. The group breathe a sigh of relief and steady themselves, thinking on the danger that still lies in wait.

Meanwhile, [[Amotti]] and [[Wild Bill]] return to the top level of [[Underton]] with Rusty Jack in tow. Determined to find a safe place his companion can shelter when the sun goes down, Wild Bill eventually investigates a station building a little way away from the Aviary. He finds the abandoned [[The Coal Track|Coal Track]] train, and identifies a carriage with a bolt lock door where Rusty Jack could hide. Amotti notes that they may still need one or more of the [[Chiropterrings]] to use as a deterrent to the Nightcrawlers, and suggests to Bill that if [[Agatha Winslow|Agatha]] took issue with this they may have to resort to taking one by force. Wild Bill strongly advises against this but begrudgingly accepts that they will need one of the animals to place next to Rusty Jack.

The two re-enter the Aviary, finding it unoccupied save for the squabbling Chiropterrings. They discuss how best to obtain one for Rusty Jack's protection. Losing patience, Amotti uses his mage hand to reach into the cage and grab one of the Chiropterrings. The rest go into a frenzy and begin to shriek violently. Wild Bill manages to calm them down by scattering some worm-feed on the floor, which distracts all but the one Amotti still holds in his invisible grip. Dismissing Amotti's actions as reckless and suppressing his rising temper, Bill moves to leave the Aviary and go find the others while they have remaining sunlight. Kneeling to open the trap door however, he stumbles and supports himself on one hand. He brings the other up to rub his eyes as his vision clouds and a rhythmic pulsing obscures his hearing…
