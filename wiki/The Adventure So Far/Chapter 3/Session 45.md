# The Events of Session 45

Against his better judgement, [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] nudges [[Wild Bill]] awake. A shiver runs down Bill's spine as he hears the horses whine outside and he begins to pace the room. He kneels to open the Aviary trapdoor and Eli interrupts him.

> *"Think about what you're doing here, Bill, [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] said there were hundreds of those things."*

> *"I'm going to save my horse."*

[[Agatha Winslow|Agatha]] goes to Bill and places her hand on his, meeting his eyes.

> *"I'm truly sorry, honey, but trust me when I say, if you go out there, there ain't no coming back."*

Bill retreats to a corner of the room and hangs his head in resignation. Eli, wishing to change the subject, presents the object from the safe to Agatha and a flicker of recognition passes her face.

> *“Hold on a second, I know what this is. I’m sure it’s part of the seal to the [[Underton Town Hall|Town Hall]] vault. Ceremonious thing really, but Mayor Colliere gave them out a while back to high-ranking townsfolk. Henry had one, bless him. Old Colliere came to the house to present him with it himself. Come to think of it, we had a few highfaluting types visit us before it all went south. Just when we was moving up in the world. I suppose Henry's must still be in the house somewhere. The Town Hall's on the level just below us - just don't expect to find no-one there.”*

Eli considers this.

> *"And what's in the vault that it should be sealed away?"*

> *“Nothing much, far as I know. Town Hall's oldest building here, some even say it was built by the Founders. When the mine was established, I think the plan was to use the old vault to store the gold they was gonna pull from the earth. I suppose the idea was that no one man would be able to lay hands on it alone, it belonged to the town - see? Safe to say they didn’t find gold when they started digging. Well, not the yellow kind anyway. What they did find was worth far more, but we don’t store it in no vault - it’s shipped right off along [[the Coal Track]].”*

Eli presses further about the seals.

> *"So how many of these are there?"*

> *"Well, let me see Agatha looks into the air and counts under her breath I'd say there must be six… yeah that sounds right… one for each council member."*

Satisfied, Eli leans back against a wall and lowers his hat. Eventually, he, Bill and Agatha drift off to sleep.

Eli wakes in the night. The aviary is dark, lit only by faint moonlight, and the [[Chiropterrings]] stir restlessly on their perches. His blood chills as he hears the sound of something large moving slowly across the roof, and the scraping of long claws on metal. Scanning the faces of his companions, he sees Agatha sitting upright - eyes wide and fixed on him. She slowly raises a finger in front of her pursed lips, and with her other hand reaches for a wrench from her tool bag. In a swift movement, she throws it at the bars of the chiropterring cage and the creatures begin to shriek. There is a scrambling on the roof and then the sound of something large hitting the ground below and scarpering off into the night. Agatha lets out a sigh of relief.

The commotion wakes [[Sel]], who moves into the cage to peer out of the small window. He sees the building on the edge of town, behind which the rail tracks disappear from view. All seems still for a moment, before some movement catches his eye. He sees a large creature moving quickly on all fours between buildings. Thinking fast, he casts a minor illusion mimicking the sound of the chiropterrings in front of the creature, and observes as it winces and stumbles before running off out of sight. He returns to the group, pondering on the potential use of this weakness, before allowing himself to fall asleep once more.

Morning comes, and the aviary is lit by a shaft of sunlight coming through the small window. Woken with renewed purpose, Bill immediately leaves through the trapdoor and is followed by Eli, Gambit and [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]]. The four make their way to the Station House. The chain remains wrapped round the front door, but there is a terrible stillness in the air. Eli hears the faint buzzing of flies, and a stale, metallic smell reaches his nostrils. Wild Bill approaches the door, and sees a pool of dried blood creeping out from underneath. He unwraps the chain and pulls the door open, only to immediately recoil and wretch at the sight inside. Pistachio hangs motionless in the air, hovering and rotating as if held in stasis. The horse's lower half has been torn off, and its entrails pool on the floor beneath it. Gambit realises with a wave of nausea that Pistachio must have been caught in the magical snare that he set before leaving the station house. The group compose themselves, and move in to investigate the building. Wild Bill's eyes are drawn to a sizeable hole in the back wall that appears to have been smashed through. Around it, he sees a blood smear indicating something having been dragged out and off elsewhere. He also sees a trail of hoof prints leading off and out into the wilderness beyond the edge of the town.

Having seen enough, Eli turns to leave. Before passing through the door, his attention is drawn by something in the upper corner of the room. Initially, he struggles to see anything. After a moment, however, he sees movement. From a dark shape in the corner, limbs extend. Long, sharp claws sink into the wooden walls, holding the creature in place as it studies him. Eli sees that one of the creature's legs hangs limp and shattered, the result of an injury. Eli meets its curious gaze, staring into its multi-panelled, insectoid eyes - lidless and black. There is a hum as a pair of concealed wings begin to vibrate and the creature opens its mandibles, before leaping from the wall straight at Eli.

Eli pulls his crossbow, but manages only to fire off a bolt into the wall. Wild Bill fires two shots off into its carapace. Gambit sends Ralf hurtling toward the creature, but he is knocked aside by its momentum. The creature bundles into Eli, and slashes at him with lethal claws. All the while, its long tails flicks from side to side, and Eli is forced to duck to avoid jabs from the wicked poisonous stinger at its tip. Frantic, the monster swipes at the group. It leaps upon Bill, viciously tearing at his body and sticking him with its venomous stinger. Bill is badly wounded in the struggle. Gambit imbues bullets from his revolver with radiant light and fires them into the creature's torso. Where they strike, the flesh begins to sizzle as if scorched and the creature drops to all fours - bounding at Gambit with furious intent. Lythara steps in, deftly smacking aside the stinger with the flat of her glaive before arcing it upward to meet the creature's neck. Its head rolls onto the floor, thick black blood coursing out of the stump of its neck, and it falls to the ground dead.

Nursing their wounds, the group rejoin the others at the aviary. Agatha expresses wonder that they managed to kill one of the nightcrawlers, but Eli warns gravely that it was only one and it was injured at that. The Players discuss their next moves. Wild Bill insists that he must follow the horses' trail and find Rusty Jack. [[Amotti]] offers to accompany him, expressing more concern for the safe return of MB. The others decide to investigate the Town Hall on the level below. Agatha explains that she will go to the Bathhouse Cave on the lowest levels to try and fetch more water, and warns the group that their only hope of survival is to return to the safety of the Aviary before nightfall. Descending the ladder, the Players say their goodbyes briefly before Amotti and Wild Bill walk off in the direction of the hoof prints.

The rest of the group descend the steps carved into the red sandstone walls of Underton and follow the curving path downward. They see that the edges of the path here are blocked by wooden picket fencing, and that below there are a series of sharp drops with other plateaus and buildings before the dark pit of the central chasm. They approach a two-storey building quite unlike the others in the town, which sits alone on its own plateau. The building, although patched and renovated in places with wood and corrugated iron, has stone foundations. They see that it likely consists of two floors, with a central portion and West and East wings branching off. Its stone walls speak of a longer history than the other hastily constructed buildings of the town, and the group stops for a while in its imposing shadow. They see that the tall double doors at the building's entrance are slightly ajar. Sel turns Eli invisible so that he might scout ahead inside, and he moves in through the doors quietly. With Eli gone, Sel and Gambit briefly discuss their respective histories. Initially, Gambit is the more inquisitive of the two, however swiftly changes the conversation when his mysterious past with Amotti is brought up.

Eli scouts the inside of the Town Hall. He enters a cavernous entrance foyer, and notes a symbol engraved on the floor - a six-spoked ship's wheel. His eyes are drawn to two motionless bodies sprawled out on the floor, both in Protectorate armour. Further into the room, he sees a grand, semi-circular reception desk carved from wood. Eli finds three drawers set into it that are open and empty. They are marked in turn with sun, moon and star motifs. The reception is flanked by twin marble staircase, curving up to an upper floor with a perimeter balcony looking down on his position. Another body, that of one of the nightcrawlers, is lying face-down on the right of the two stairways. Before returning to the others, Eli does a quick check of the room. He sees that this lower floor has two entrances leading into the West and East wings. The upper floor similarly has two doors leading West and East, but also has a more ornate door of solid metal in the centre of the back wall. He returns to the others outside.

After hearing Eli's report, Sel, Gambit, Caeldrid, Lythara and Silas follow him into the Town Hall. Dust hangs in the air, stale with the smell of death. Dim light filters in from small cracks in the old stone walls. A wind swells up from the chasm far behind them and the entrance doors creak as it passes through, willing them to step inside and explore…
