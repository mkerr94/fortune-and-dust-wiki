# The Events of Session 46

[[Elias Blackwood|Eli]], [[Gambit]], [[Sel]], [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]], [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] and [[Silas Seer|Silas]] step into the [[Underton Town Hall|town hall]].  They enter the cavernous reception foyer, lit by the eerie glow of a gas-burning chandelier hanging from the ceiling high above. On the floor beneath, an image of a six-spoked ship’s wheel is carved into the wood. Motes of dust twist in the low light as air is pulled and pushed from outside through small cracks in the old stone foundations, in and out, as if the building itself was taking heavy, slow breaths in the silence. The stale smell of death hangs in the air. On the floor there are two dead soldiers of the Protectorate, their armour torn and ragged.

To their left and right, they can just make out what look like entranceways to the West and East wings, respectively. Directly ahead, they see the impressive semi-circular reception desk. It sits directly between the two curving stone staircases leading to the upper floor. Sprawled on the right of these two staircases is a bigger body, that of one of the creatures. The area behind the desk and staircases is somewhat obscured in shadow.

Caeldrid illuminates the dark area behind the reception desk with the rippling green light from his sword. Moving into it, the Players find the huge entrance to the Town Hall vault. It is a square slab of thick metal, set into stone foundations at a forty-five degree angle to the floor. It has a hexagonal inset with six sections, one of which Eli confirms fits the triangular object he found in the Aviary, and a long barred handle that can be twisted to open the seal. Eli leans down and knocks forcefully on the vault entrance, proceeding to listen for signs of movement behind the door. He hears nothing.

The group resolve to explore the rest of the Town Hall, starting with the lower West Wing. They go to the door on the west side of the foyer, and find that it has been blocked by an iron shutter which has since been torn and destroyed - allowing it to be easily removed and the door to be passed through. Pulling away the destroyed iron shutter and moving through the door, they find themselves in a dark corridor. The gas lamps lining the walls here seem to have been extinguished, and there is a cloying smell of damp in the air. The stale silence is broken by the sound of water dripping from the ceiling. The corridor stretches off to their left and right. To the left, there seems to be a door at the end of the hallway. To the right, Gambit thinks he can make out the beginning of a set of stairs presumably leading to the Upper floor.

They decide to investigate the door at the end of the corridor first. They find that it is made of thick oak, and is surrounded by an elaborate stone doorframe topped by a symbol of a Moon. A bronze plaque on the door reads "Council Chamber". Caeldrid finds a circular key-hole set into the door which also bears a Moon motif. Surmising that they must need a Moon key, likely missing from the reception desk drawer marked with a Moon that Eli found earlier, they instead decide to simply pick the lock. Eli does so with considerable skill, and the group move inside the room beyond.

They enter a room lit by a warm fire burning in the centre of an ornate mantlepiece in the left-hand wall. The centre of the room is dominated by a large, circular table made of dark wood. It is surrounded by six chairs. In the largest of the chairs, situated at the head of the table, sits a figure. Caeldrid approaches cautiously, glowing sword raised. He sees that the figure is a young woman, of shoulder length brown hair. She wears a simple frock and has a pendant chain around her neck. Her head hangs limply, chin pressed to her chest. Caeldrid nudges her, and her body slumps onto the table with a thud. A rank stench is released, and Caeldrid sees with horror that her back has been split at the spine as if opened like a book. Inside is an empty cavity where the internal organs should be, giving off a rotten smell.

The thud of the body hitting the table stirs something in the corner of the room. Eli turns. From the corner of the room, he sees a huge creature drop and slowly rise to its feet until it stands at full height. It makes an insectoid clicking sound from its mandibles and a low hum fills the room as its wings begin to vibrate. Silas draws his scimitar, shaking, and backs up against the far wall in terror. The creature takes a step toward Lythara and attacks.

Lythara is locked in combat with the creature. Despite fighting it off with her glaive, she is stuck and badly wounded by its poisonous stinger. Caeldrid leaps onto the table and tries to draw the monster off, swinging his shining sword in large overhead swipes. Its attention drawn to him, the creature strikes him also with its barbed stinger, sinking it into a gap in his plate armour. Behind him, Silas lets out a yelp as the corpse of the young woman stands upright. Her eyes stare vacantly and her head hangs to the side. As if controlled by unseen strings, she stretches her hands out toward Silas and staggers towards him, teeth bared. She stumbles into him and sinks a sickening bite into his shoulder as he struggles to push her off. All the while, Eli and Gambit try to pierce the creature with bolts and bullets from their respective crossbow and pistol. In each instance, the missiles glance harmlessly off of the thick chitinous carapace covering the monster's body.

Sensing the tide turning against the group, Caeldrid takes out his [[Bottle of Sunlight]]. Saying a silent prayer to his goddess and hoping against hope, he smashes the bottle onto the creature's head. From the point of contact, a burst of brilliant, blinding sunlight floods the room. The creature hisses and squeals, and steam begins to rise from its body as its outer shell begins to sizzle. Regaining his courage and pushing past all of the others in their light-induced daze, Silas jumps onto the table and rushes the monster with his scimitar. His blade passes straight through the once-solid cuticle, which crumbles as it is struck. The scimitar embeds into the creature's shoulder and it staggers back, head stretched to the ceiling. Its neck exposed, Eli sinks two crossbow bolts into its throat and it sinks to its knees before falling face-first onto the floor - dead.

Meanwhile, [[Wild Bill]] and [[Amotti]] stand over the hoof-prints at the hole in the rear Station House wall. They decide to follow them as far as it takes, and set out once again into the expanse of dry and dusty plain beyond [[Underton]]'s outskirts. Walking for a few miles, they begin to discuss each other's histories. Amotti asks where Wild Bill got his name from, explaining that his name (Xai) comes from the noble Drow house of his lineage. Wild Bill claims no such grandeur, relaying that the nickname 'Wild' was simply picked up in his younger days of recklessness and misbehaviour. He goes on to describe his upbringing on a farm with his parents, where he developed his love for horses. Picking up on this, the pair then discuss their respective parentage. They ponder if, when the dust settles on their latest adventure, whether either of them will truly have anything to go back to. Wild Bill presses Amotti on his relationship with Gambit. Amotti explains that, when he fled the Underdark and went into hiding from his sister, he lived from meal to meal and was pained by the constant sunlight of the world above. When Gambit found him, he crafted for him a hood that offered protection from the light, which Amotti credits with saving his life.

The two eventually reach the red-stone gullies they previously passed through which occupy the landscape between the open plains before Underton and the tall prairie grass surrounding the abandoned gatehouse. Wild Bill notices that the hoof-prints here indicate that the horse they are tracking slowed to a trot. Amotti blurts out that he hopes they find MB. Wild Bill reiterates that he cares only for Rusty Jack's safety, that he has been his only good friend in this world. Amotti retorts.

> *"They say only the good die young."*

> *"In that case, Amotti, I think you'll live until you're old and grey."*

Wild Bill sucks in a long breath and enters the gully. Turning a corner, they see the small shrine dedicated to the Craftsman that they passed on their way to Underton. Beside it, panting and eyes white with panic, is Rusty Jack. Bill embraces his old friend as his heart swells with relief. The two discuss what to do now for Rusty Jack's ongoing safety. Wild Bill states a desire to take him back to the stables at Fortuna, where he knows he will be safe. Pointing out the great distance and the imminent danger to their friends, Amotti thinks this is not feasible. After a lengthy and heated discussion, the two eventually decide to take Rusty Jack back to Underton and figure out how to keep him safe when there.

Back in the Council Chamber, the body of the young woman, which collapsed to the floor in the burst of bottled sunlight, begins to convulse. Eli sees several small, dark shapes leaving it and scurrying off into the corners of the room. It then lies still and lifeless. Eli wonders if something was using her body as a host. Wanting to ensure it stays dead, Lythara stabs the head of the corpse. She then examines the remains and notes, with an uncomfortable lurch in her stomach, that the breakages in the woman's spine and rear ribcage were made from the inside out.

Kneeling by the body of the [[The Nightcrawlers|Nightcrawler]], Caeldrid tries to scoop some of the concentrated residue from the Bottle of Sunlight off of its head. To his surprise, he finds that it has solidified, seemingly in reaction to its exposure to air. Battered and bloodied, the group decide to rest for an hour in the Council Chamber. Caeldrid and Lythara discuss the brutality of the creature they fought, and how desperate the situation became facing only one of the hundreds Gwynevere saw moving through the town at night. Lythara poses the question on everyone's mind.

> *"How do we survive if we meet more than one?"*

Caeldrid responds grimly.

> *"We don't".*
