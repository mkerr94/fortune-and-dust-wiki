# The Events of Session 43

Having sliced the foreleg of one of the attacking coyote pack clean off as it jumped through the window of the abandoned shack, [[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]] spins her glaive above her head and brings the blade down on the beast's head. At the front door, the hulking alpha coyote breaks through the log barrier and stares down [[Wild Bill]] hungrily. Saliva dripping from its jaw, it leaps for him and slashes at his chest. He manages to fend it off, however another member of the pack raises its hackles ready to strike. Suddenly, from the top of the stairs, two crossbow bolts come whistling through the air and sink into the coyote's neck - dropping it to the ground. Bill glances up the steps to see [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] at the top, and the two exchange a respectful nod.

[[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]] engages the coyotes at the rear windows. He deflects one of their bites with his sword, bringing the blade round to pierce directly through the coyote's mouth as it goes for a second bite. He spins and pushes away the other, cleaving its skull with an overhead swing. Its pack gone, the alpha looks around in desperation. The group look on as it begins to rise into the air and flails its legs helplessly. They turn to see [[Sel]], having climbed through one of the bottom windows, focused intently on the creature and holding it in stasis. Bill fires to bullets into its side and it whelps, blood dripping from its ripped fur. Hearing the creature whimper, Lythara ends its misery with a magical firebolt. Sel drops the smoking carcass to the ground.

He investigates the remains, looking specifically for signs of the black corruption that caused the mutations in [[Clement's Hollow]]. He finds none. To compound this, Eli and Caeldrid observe and discuss the unlikeliness that one of the coyotes would be able to scale the cabin wall and drag someone from the upper room. Wild Bill kneels by the alpha.

> "These were just simple animals. Hungry and drawn by the smell of death…"

[[Amotti]], spinning his dancing lights, notes also how the animals did not seem to be affected by  their glow - meaning the "stay in the light" warning they found was likely not related to the coyotes. He sends the lights out over the grass sea, helping the group see out over the prairie. The stars shine above and the moon sits in silent judgement. A breeze ripples through the blades of grass and all seems still. With the immediate threat gone, Amotti suggests summoning his magical Adequate Abode to shelter the group. He does so and they rest, with Eli and Lythara presiding over uneventful watches. The night passes, and the first rays of the sun creep over the eastern horizon. Eli gives the group another few hours rest, before they stir and begin to pack the wagon.

Amotti notes that, during the night, [[Silas Seer|Silas]] did not sleep. He follows him outside and finds him kneeling next to the corpse of the Protectorate soldier.

> *"I can't stop seeing his face, [[Braskin Dropstone|Braskin]]. He didn't even hesitate, standing between me and those things. He didn't deserve what happened to him. None of them deserved that. The only time I've ever felt truly part of something, and I'll never see them again. I don't regret what we did to Ares, I would do it again and I'll accept the consequences, but that doesn't make it any easier."*

Amotti reassures Silas, offering him a small handful of dirt for protection.

> *"You did the right thing for the greater good, Silas, for the balance. It may not seem so right now, but you'll make peace with it one day. Sometimes, we have to do something that feels wrong to achieve what is right."*

> *"And could you do the same? If it came to it?"*

> *"I already have. I killed a dear friend of mine, the leader of my order. I did it to save many. In return, I was blessed with the gifts of [[Eilistraee]]."*

> *"Well, [[The Warrior]] has not seen fit yet to grant me any gifts of his, but I thank you for your kindness Amotti."*

> *"We could use you, Silas, if we are to help those at the mine we will have need of every strong hand we can find."*

> *"I'm afraid I'm no hero. They say the train there can move at the speed of one hundred horses, I intend to use it to leave this place as quickly as I can."*

Silas turns to the group.

> *"In [[Underton]], am I really to suppose you want to help the people there out of the goodness of your hearts?"*

Lythara and Caeldrid look to each other, and reveal to Silas that they are looking for the rogue [[Marismente|Marismentan]] militia. He tells them everything else that he knows.

> *"It was need-to-know, and I guess we didn't need to know, but the brothers talk. Command had been tracking them for weeks. Marismentan militia have been targeting infrastructure for years, the railways and such like. Some say they even have designs on the harbour at [[Portumluna]]. The higher-ups thought this group were going to attack the ironworks in Clement's Hollow, even sent a few of our men to warn the townsfolk there. Didn't come to anything in the end, they were sighted nearby but no attacks.*
> 
> *Later though, [[Ghrist Skylark]] spotted them trying to pass by Fortuna. We intercepted them, but one stayed behind to guard the others retreat. Powerful, strange magic. Dangerous. We put her in the lock-up but orders came in from Command, she had to be sent to Portumluna immediately and then on to the capital for questioning. Someone with a lot of power, and a lot of money, has a stake in this somehow. I wonder if the rest of the militia made it to Underton, if they could be behind whatever has happened there?"*

The Players ask about the group of Marismente. While Silas was not directly involved, he explains that there were rumours from the soldiers sent to confront them. They reported a strange member of the group, much smaller in height. The female who stayed behind to guard the other's escape seemed very willing to lay down her life to do so. They numbered fewer than ten. In response to this threat and to the strange correspondence from Underton, [[Captain Josias Bartolomeu|Josias]] sent nearly all of his men to pursue the Marismente to Underton.

The Players continue through the prairie grass trail. Several miles up the road, as the sun begins to crest in the sky, Eli spots birds circling overhead. Beneath them, the group find the body of another [[The Protectorate|Protectorate]] soldier, an elven female. Although he doesn't know her by name, Silas recognises her as one of the group sent to Underton in pursuit of the [[The Rogue Marismente|Rogue Marismente]]. Her body has been largely eaten by animals. Her right hand is outstretched toward a dark patch in the ground surrounded by shards of brown glass. Eli notes that they match the [[Bottle of Sunlight]] found in [[The Abandoned Gatehouse]].

They continue along the trail. The tall prairie grass on either side of begins to thin. Ahead, they see the trail seems to dip below surface level. As they reach the edge of the prairie, the landscape shifts into one of red-stone gullies and shallow canyons. The trail continues on a slight decline as the sandstone rises on either side.

Pressing forward, the Players begin to see signs of human presence along the path. Where the walls of the gully have been eroded or threaten to collapse, wooden struts reinforced with iron have been carefully placed to support it. Following these, they see an alcove that seems to have been carved from the wall of the gully. It houses what looks to be a small shrine, inside which a weathered stone statuette stands vigil. It depicts a young dwarven figure, holding a pick-axe and shovel crossed in front of him. At his feet, there are some empty wooden bowls and some rusted coins. Eli leaves a few coins of his own as offering and Silas questions his motives.

> *"You worship [[The Craftsman]]?"*

> *"Not personally, but perhaps he may offer us some protection nonetheless."*

> *"In the Protectorate, we are taught that the Warrior watches over those who defend the defenceless. I wonder if the Craftsman did the same for the miners at Underton?"*

The trail eventually leaves the shadows of the sandstone gullies and opens out onto a dusty plain. Silhouetted against the afternoon sun, the Players see the uneven outline of buildings in the distance. Scanning the town skyline, Eli notices something else. Seemingly billowing from atop one of the roofs, there is a column of smoke. It rises into the air like a warning, or an invitation…

The buildings of the town come into focus as they draw nearer. Something here is wrong. There is a thick silence blanketing everything, pierced only by the cawing of carrion crows, and in the air there is a cloying stench of decay.

Scanning the row of buildings lining the entrance to the town, they see a tall windmill to the far left. It is motionless, its sails torn and ragged and their support struts destroyed. It stands next to another tall structure with a large, thick, iron box at its top connected to a system of pullies and crates. Beside it, in front of an expansive warehouse, there is fenced-off stable yard with no horses.

To the far right, the Players see something that they haven't yet encountered a short distance away. A set of thick iron rails on the ground which lead from behind another building and stretch off into the distance in the direction they have just come from.

Directly in front of the group sit two buildings, one raised from the ground by supports and accessed by a ladder, the other a substantial two-storey structure with a slanted roof. It is from this roof that the smoke rises.

Moving round the side of the building, Wild Bill finds that the acrid stench becomes stronger. Between this building and its neighbours, he sees that multi-coloured bunting has been hung. In places it has fallen, and flaps listlessly in the light breeze. Moving round the side and out in front of the building, Bill sees carrion crows startle and rise off in flight from something on the ground. As his eyes follow their flight path upward, he sees two tall posts, a welcome gate of sorts, between which a huge canvas sign has been hung. In paint, it reads:

> *"Happy Highharvestide"*

Below it is a sight that turns Bill's stomach, as the breeze fills his nostrils with the scent of death. Dried blood stains the ground. Below the signposts, there is a scattering of viscera and dismembered body parts. Pulling his bandana over his mouth and nose, Bill is able to move closer. Arms, legs, heads, intestines and torsos, all cleaved from their original owners litter the ground. On closer inspection, Bill sees that some of these poor souls were clad in Protectorate uniform. Studying the scene closer, he sees to his horror that they seem to have been arranged in a pattern, in an almost ritualistic manner.

Meanwhile, Sel levitates Eli enough to pull himself onto the roof of the two-storey building. He scans the surface, and sees the fire causing the smoke. He confirms it has been deliberately constructed. Noticing Wild Bill's movements below, he moves toward the edge of the roof for a closer look at the ground below. With the right perspective, Eli sees that the pattern of the body parts is a crude approximation of a familiar symbol. It is that of two encircling catfish - one endlessly pursuing the other…
