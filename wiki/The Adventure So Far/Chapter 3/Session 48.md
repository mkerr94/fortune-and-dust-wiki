# The Events of Session 48

In the Aviary, the frantic cries of the [[Chiropterrings]] ring out in the pale afternoon sun…

[[Lythara Sunstrider|Lythara]], [[Sel]], [[Gambit]], [[wiki/People & Organisations/Player Characters/Caeldrid Àrmann an Gealach|Caeldrid]], [[Elias Blackwood|Eli]] and [[Silas Seer|Silas]] (having defeated [[The Reanimated]] [[The Protectorate|Protectorate]] corpses guarding the West Wing Corridor stairs, decide to rest a while in the Council Chamber before ascending to the Upper Floor. Beside the smouldering embers in the fireplace, Eli sits and lights his wooden pipe - ruminating between draws on all he has seen.

The group then return to the West Wing corridor stairs and climb them to the Upper Floor. At the top, they find a door leading out to the Main Foyer 2F.

*"You push through the door at the top of the staircase. The Upper West Balcony is lit by the glow of the central chandelier. There is a landing at the top of the stairs which leads to the East balcony. At its centre, set into the back wall, is a large iron door - different in construction to the others you have seen. Along the balcony, the edge is blocked by an iron railing inlaid at regular intervals with six distinctive symbols (the watchful sword, the crossed tools, the arcane hand, the captured flame, the offered chalice, the opened book).*

*Halfway along the wall, there is a thick door. It is set with a Sun symbol and a plaque reading ‘Archive (lower)’. Water is pooling near its base. Next to it, a caged spiral iron staircase leads to a small platform above you which seems to house another door. At the end of the balcony walkway, there is a glass cabinet of some sort - decorative in construction."*

Realising that the cannot yet open the doors to the Upper or Lower Archive without the Sun Key, the Players instead examine the glass display cabinet at the end of the West Wing Balcony. They see that, inside, there is a marble sculpture depicting a hand with outstretched palm. The palm is surrounded by several arcane symbols. Eli asks Sel to examine it for magical aura and, wishing to save his strength, Sel instead pretends to close his eyes and simply examines the case without incantation. He sees a small latch at the bottom right-hand corner and, distracting Eli momentarily, unhooks it. The glass case retracts on hinges, freeing its contents, and Sel declares that he has magically dispelled the seal protecting it. Simultaneously, Gambit incants Detect Magic and finds no magical pulses emanating from the object. Eli pockets the plush velvet cushion upon which the sculpture sits and Caeldrid retrieves the hand itself, remarking:

> *"That's sure to come in handy…"*

The Players then move across the Upper Floor to its central portion.

*"Against the back wall of the Upper Floor, in the centre of the landing fed by the twin staircases leading down to the Foyer, there is an ornate door made of iron. In intricate ridges along its face, you see that the decorations show the same six-spoked ship's wheel symbol as the floor below. At the end of each spoke sit distinctive motifs, that of the watchful sword, the crossed tools, the arcane hand, the captured flame, the offered chalice and the opened book.* 

*The door is served by a single circular handle secured to the centre. There are no discernible keyholes and, when pulled, the door and handle do not budge."*

Realising they cannot yet enter the room beyond the iron door, the Players instead explore the East Wing balcony.

*"The Upper East Wing balcony is a mirror image of the West across the room. In the centre of the balcony walkway, there is a doorway. The door looks to have been smashed. At the end of the walkway, there is a stately grandfather clock, antiquated in design and elegance.*

*The clock has a panel in its body which, when opened, reveals a vacant hook where a weight would normally sit. At its head, behind a veil of crystal, its face glints in the light of the chandelier. It is decorated with a shiny copper plate, into which a relief appearing to show twin maps of Faerun and Alcadeña has been etched. Two brass clock hands occupy the fore, with the long minute hand ending in an ornate brass sun."*

Lythara inspects the clock closer, and sees that the minute hand is in fact the Sun Key. She first tries to smash its crystal face with her crowbar, but suffers a retaliatory blow of magical force. She then tries to manually depress the weight in the body of the clock, again to no avail. Instead, the Players go through the smashed door and descend a set of stairs beyond to the East Wing Corridor.

*"Carefully making your way down the steps, you enter a corridor lit by the soft glow of gas lanterns. They sit in regularly spaced alcoves at head height along the corridor, light emanating from within. As you move down the hallway, you come to a set of doors opposite each other. The one on your right, you surmise leads back to the Foyer. The one on your left is collapsed and caved in, blocking entry. Rubble litters the floor of the hallway around it.*

*At the end of the corridor is another door, surrounded by decorative stone cornicing. The door is made of thick oak, and engraved with the same ship's wheel as the Foyer floor. At the tip of each spoke are depicted the same six symbols you saw on the Upper floor railings. At the tip of the cornicing is a motif depicting a shining star."*

Noting that a more convenient escape route may be useful, Lythara opens the door back out to the Main Foyer first, which causes the shutter on the other side to retract. She then turns her attention to the rubble in the collapsed doorway behind her, looking for anything significant. Though the doorway has collapsed to the point of completely blocking entry, she finds a keystone that likely sat at the top of the archway which bears a Star symbol.

The group then use the Star Key to open the door at the end of the corridor and enter the room beyond, noting before they do that to their left there is a nook housing a narrow stone spiral staircase which leads upward to another room. They enter the room beyond the Star door.

*"There is an odd, reverent silence in this room, as when one enters an empty church hall. A long, red rug in the centre splits the room in two, forming a pathway to a raised dais at the far end. Atop this dais, you can see an altar, upon which a heavy-looking iron chest rests.*

*Lining either side of the central walkway, there are six life-sized statues made from white marble. Facing each other as if at the side of a procession, their unmoving faces gaze out into the room. They observe the room with unseeing eyes, each held forever in a proud pose, demanding humility before their authority."*

The Players study the six statues in turn, and find that each is marked by a plaque at the base identifying the figures depicted.

[[The Warrior]] - a strong, broad Orc male holding clasped hands in front of him as if to grip the hilt of a sword

[[The Craftsman]] - a stout Dwarf male holding hands crossed before him

[[The Scholar]] - a graceful elven female in flowing robes holding hands palm facing upward before her

[[The Sorcerer]] - a scruffy halfling female clutching a large tome in one arm while the other is stretched out in front of her, missing the hand at the end

[[The Servant]] - a thin, drawn human male with a sharp nose upon which half-moon spectacles sit, he holds his hands cupped in front of him

[[The Scientist]] - a wiry gnome male with ever searching eyes, he holds one hand outstretched as one would to hold a lantern in the darkness

Caeldrid steps up to the statue of the Sorcerer and slots the marble hand onto the corresponding slot on its wrist. Lythara then finds a similar marble sculpture of a crossed pick-axe and shovel and places this into the vacant hands of the Craftsman statue. Meanwhile, Eli investigates the iron chest and finds that it is affixed to the stone altar and has no discernible seams or latch. He surmises that it's opening is likely connected to the missing pieces of the other statues.

Stepping back into the East Wing corridor, Eli notices that one of the alcoves containing the gas lanterns has no light coming from it. Investigating, he finds that it instead contains a small marble sculpture of an open lantern inside which a small marble flame sits. He returns to the Ceremony Room and places it in the outstretched hand of the statue of the Scientist.

Satisfied and setting out with the aim of collecting the remaining missing pieces, the Players ascend the stone spiral staircase next to the Ceremony Room entrance. They find themselves stepping out into the upper floor of a large room.

*"You find yourself on the upper floor of a huge Assembly Hall. Down a few steps in front of you, there is staggered seating offering a view of the hall and dais below. To your right, you can see the top of another enclosed spiral staircase that seems to lead to the lower floor.  Ahead of you, there is a small walkway ending in a platform situated directly over the raised dais on the lower floor. On it sits a metallic cabinet with a crystal top for display. To the right of this, three iron chain pulleys stretch from boxes on the floor and up into the roof.*

*Looking to the floor below, you see row upon row of benches, facing the dais at the far end of the room at a slightly inward-facing angle. There are unmoving figures slumped in sporadic positions about the seating. On the dais, there is an altar which sticks out of the ground and a lectern for delivering speeches. Below the benches, you can make out a complex mural painted into the floor.*

*The mural appears to show two distinct layers of earth: the topsoil and below. On the section nearest the dais, a forest of trees is depicted above ground. One towers above the rest, an elm tree proud and ancient. Beneath the forest floor, the painting morphs into an abstract depiction of the space between spaces, the transition between above and below. The two worlds are anchored by a painting of the ship's wheel symbol in the interstitial space. Below the soil, the mural gets stranger still. In the subterranean world, graceful catfish swim amongst a city carved in stone. They swim through the streets toward a central structure, a sanctuary in the deep."*

Caeldrid descends the stairs to explore the lower floor while Lythara and the others investigate the display cabinet and chains at the far end of the upper floor. Lythara sees that, inside the cabinet, there is a weight for the grandfather clock decorated with a brass sun. She sees that the cabinet has a codex lock with three dials. Above each dial in turn is depicted a sun, moon and star - matching symbols above the three chains to her right. Spinning one of the dials, she sees that they too have symbols: a ship's wheel, a tree, and a catfish.

Lythara pulls the Sun chain. There is a scraping noise from above as an aperture in the roof of the room opens and illuminates a spot on the mural below in a shaft of sunlight. It marks the ship's wheel on the mural. She turns the Sun dial to the symbol of the ship's wheel.

As she pulls the Moon chain, the corpses of the Protectorate sat in reverence begin to rise and surround Caeldrid. He sees the symbol upon which the new shaft of light is falling is obscured underneath one of the benches, and cannot be made out. Lythara quickly guesses catfish for the Moon dial and tree for Star dial but finds the cabinet does not open. Meanwhile, Caeldrid is beset on all sides by the Reanimated. Gambit sends Ralf to assist, but in his attempt to jump from the upper floor to the lower he slips and crashes to the ground. One of the Reanimated grabs Caeldrid but, as he steps into the shaft of light above the ship's wheel symbol, the arms of the monster begin to sizzle and burn. He seizes his chance and pushes the creature off, before igniting the lower floor with a blast of flame from his sword. The creatures and some of the benches erupt in flame, but continue their advance.

Sel utters a guttural incantation under his breath and, from the upper floor, sends a blast of arcane flame down to engulf the remaining Reanimated. Amid the flame, the last standing creature advances toward Caeldrid, who has tired from his sustained wounds and lifts his sword toward it from a kneeling position in one trembling hand. Just as it is about to reach him, Gambit drops it with one shot from his revolver, and it falls onto Caeldrid's outstretched blade. He stands, slides the creature off, and breathes a sigh of relief.
